# Chapter 8: Endorsed standards and adjacent NRC guidance (name-only)

Sources: Spec Excluded table X1-X16 (no source pages). This chapter is a name-only orientation. No standard text, no URLs, and no reproduced guidance body appear here. Editions are exactly as the binding Excluded table lists them. Adjacent current NRC documents outside this pack are named only.

## Core Idea

The six July 2013 Regulatory Guides and BTP 7-14 Rev 6 cite IEEE, IEC, and IAEA materials by designation. Those texts are copyrighted or rights-reserved, so this pack never reproduces their clauses. Chapter 08 records designation, edition as cited, owner, status, what cites them, and where the pack points a reader who needs the name. It also names adjacent NRC documents that sit outside the pack body by scope ruling, and states the NUREG/CR contractor-report exclusion rule.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Name-only citation rule.** Designation and edition only. No clause text, no tables from the standard, no URLs.
- **Edition rule.** Cite the edition printed in the RG or BTP that a chapter draws from. Do not silently upgrade to a later edition. The plant licensing basis, not this pack, decides which revision binds a given plant.
- **Pack body versus orientation.** ch02-ch07 restate NRC staff positions and may name the endorsed standard. This chapter holds the orientation map for X1-X16.

## Key Concepts

- **Endorsement is not incorporation of the whole standard text into this pack.** An RG may endorse an IEEE standard with exceptions; the pack restates the NRC exceptions, not the IEEE clauses.
- **X14 and X15 are NRC documents outside pack body.** RG 1.152 Rev 4 and BTP 7-19 Rev 9 are current adjacent positions; they are named here so a reader knows they exist, not so this pack restates them.
- **X16 is a family exclusion.** Contractor NUREG/CR reports need a written per-document determination before any pack reuse. The worked example is NUREG/CR-6463, a software-languages report, not a V&V handbook.
- **IEEE Std 603-1991 stays at 1991 in this pack.** Later IEEE editions exist; the 2013 family and BTP cite 1991 for the safety-system criteria path this pack uses.
- **IEEE Std 7-4.3.2-2003 is the family citation.** RG 1.152 Rev 4's 2016 endorsement is a different slice and is out of pack body.

## Mental Models

- **ch08 is the card catalog, not the library.** It tells you the call number and the shelf rule. The books stay with their owners.
- **Pack chapters point here for edition spelling.** When a life cycle or review chapter names an endorsed standard, the edition string matches this table.
- **Licensing basis beats pack pin for plant work.** This pack pins July 2013 RGs and BTP 7-14 Rev 6 for reconstruction. A plant committed to another revision is reviewed against that commitment.

## Anti-patterns

- **Pasting IEEE, IEC, or IAEA clause text into pack notes.** Name-only means name-only.
- **Upgrading 1012-2004 to a later 1012, or 603-1991 to a later 603, inside pack prose.** Edition as cited wins.
- **Treating RG 1.152 Rev 4 or BTP 7-19 Rev 9 as if this pack covers them.** They are adjacent, not in body.
- **Assuming every NUREG/CR is public-domain staff guidance.** Contractor title pages block auto-clearance until a written determination exists.
- **Using NUREG/CR-6463 as if it were a V&V methods handbook.** It is a software-languages report.

## Key Takeaways

1. X1-X9 name the IEEE editions the 2013 RG family and BTP cite; each maps to a life cycle or framework chapter for name-only use.
2. X10-X12 name IEC 60880 ed. 2.0:2006, IEC 61513 ed. 2.0:2011, and IEC 62138 ed. 2.0:2018 as paywalled orientation only.
3. X13 keeps IAEA guides such as SSG-39 citation-only until a written reuse grant is confirmed per document.
4. X14 names RG 1.152 Rev 4 (July 2023, ML23054A463) as a current adjacent NRC document outside this pack.
5. X15 names BTP 7-18 Rev 6, BTP 7-19 Rev 9 (May 2024), and App 7.1-D Rev 1 as out of pack body; BTP 7-19 Rev 9 is the current diversity and common-cause failure position named here.
6. X16 excludes NUREG/CR contractor reports pending per-document determination; NUREG/CR-6463 (SoHar Inc., June 1996) is the software-languages example and not a V&V handbook.
7. No URLs and no standard text appear in this chapter or elsewhere in the pack for these designations.
