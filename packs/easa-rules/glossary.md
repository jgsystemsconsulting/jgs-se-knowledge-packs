# Glossary: easa-rules

Terms as the pinned EASA sources use them, with the chapters that carry the detail. AMC meaning first; the paywalled industry standards are name only, not reproduced.

- **AEH (airborne electronic hardware)**: Electronic hardware installed on type-certified aircraft, engines, and propellers, or used in ETSO articles. AMC 20-152A answers the AEH question: it applies to AEH contributing to hardware DAL A, B, or C functions, and its use is not required at DAL D. → ch01, ch05, ch06
- **AltMoC (alternative means of compliance)**: The escape valve both AMCs state: compliance with the AMC is not mandatory, and an applicant may use an alternative means that meets the relevant requirements, ensures an equivalent level of safety (equivalent software safety on the 115D side), and is approved by EASA on a product or ETSO article basis. Not a free pass around the safety bar. → ch01, ch02, ch05
- **AMC 20-193**: Name-only pointer for the multi-core means of compliance; not fetched and not summarised in this release. → ch01
- **CBA (circuit board assembly)**: A board or board set at DAL A/B/C that contains complex custom or complex COTS devices; CBA-1 requires requirements capture, validation, verification, configuration management, and requirement flow-down so the CBA performs its intended function. It may be defined with the equipment-level process when relevant. → ch01, ch06
- **CIA (change impact analysis)**: The analysis run when software is modified: identify the changes, run the change analyses the recognised standard associates with software change, perform the verification it indicates, and summarise results in the PSAC or the SAS. GM1 fixes the content: baseline identification, change summary, problem-report and change-request lists, new-function activations, then impact and activities across eight change item classes. → ch03, ch04, ch07
- **COTS device**: A commercial off-the-shelf semiconductor device used at DAL A/B/C (a limited objective set at DAL C). The section 6 objectives (COTS-1 to COTS-8) apply to devices classified complex and cover the ECMP, off-specification use, microcode ownership, errata, failure-mode feed into system safety, usage verification including interfaces, unused-function isolation, and critical-configuration-setting protection. Hard IP manufactured into FPGA/PLD silicon is a COTS-device matter, not COTS IP. → ch01, ch06
- **COTS IP**: Commercial IP in Soft, Firm, or Hard form inserted into a custom device at DAL A/B/C. Objectives IP-1 to IP-7 cover selection, provider and data assessment, complementary assurance for provider gaps, a three-layer verification strategy, planning, requirements and validation, and ED-80/DO-254 Appendix B expectations at DAL A/B (ED-80/DO-254: name only, not reproduced). → ch01, ch06
- **Custom device**: A PLD, FPGA, or ASIC; in ED-80/DO-254 terms a custom micro-coded component (name only, not reproduced). Simple only when a technical assessment supports complete deterministic verification under all foreseeable operating conditions with no anomalous behaviour; otherwise complex, taking the standard plus the AMC clarifications in sections 5.5 to 5.11. An assembly of simple items may itself be complex. → ch01, ch06
- **DAL (development assurance level)**: The level system safety assigns from failure-condition severity. AMC 20-115D carries the software side (levels A to D, with legacy editions mapped through the AMC's Table 1); AMC 20-152A coverage is bounded by hardware DAL A, B, and C, with a limited objective set at DAL C and use not required at DAL D. → ch02, ch03, ch05
- **DAH (design approval holder)**: One of the three audience roles both AMCs address, beside applicants and developers of airborne systems and equipment. → ch02, ch05
- **EAR (Easy Access Rules) versus official annex**: The EAR AMC-20 compilation (Amendment 23, June 2023) is the free-to-read retrieval carrier this pack reads the two AMC sections from; it is marked not an official publication. The official instruments are the ED Decisions and their annexes: ED Decision 2017/020/R and ED Decision 2020/010/R supply the decision number and date that anchor the means of compliance. This pack cites the EAR pages and the decision anchors, and never paraphrases the decision bodies as AMC text. → ch01, ch05
- **ECMP (electronic component management plan)**: The COTS-2 process covering selection, qualification, configuration management, and access to manuals, datasheets, errata, and change data; at DAL A/B, complex selection also weighs device maturity and mitigates identified risks. Industry ECMP standards may support it. → ch06
- **ETSO (European technical standard order)**: The ETSO authorisation path sits inside both AMC doors, not in a separate means: software in an ETSO article belongs to AMC 20-115D and AEH in an ETSO article to AMC 20-152A, exactly as for product certification. AltMoC and AMC-based approval are granted on a product or ETSO article basis. → ch01, ch02, ch05
- **GM (general clarification material)**: AMC 20-115D's GM1 to GM3: the CIA content model, the data-coupling and control-coupling split, and design-level error handling with runtime protection preferred at Levels A and B. → ch04
- **MoC (means of compliance)**: The acceptable means an ED Decision names for showing compliance with airworthiness requirements. Non-binding by the decisions' own framing, so the AMC is one path and AltMoC is the supervised alternative. The software MoC is AMC 20-115D; the AEH MoC is AMC 20-152A. → ch01, ch02, ch03
- **OPR (open problem report)**: Open problems with potential safety impact gate the legacy modify/reuse path through the usage-history check and count among the process deficiencies the section 5 reuse test examines. AMC 20-189 is the name-only pointer for open-problem hygiene. → ch03, ch07
- **PDH (previously developed hardware)**: Hardware reused under ED-80/DO-254 Section 11.1 through objective CD-12 (name only, not reproduced): modification, a change of function, use, or failure-condition classification, or a design-environment change can each invalidate prior credit until reassessment. → ch06
- **PHAC (Plan for Hardware Aspects of Certification)**: The planning document, or related plan, that records the process and activities intended to satisfy AMC 20-152A objectives and is submitted for certification. It carries classification calls and justifications (CD-1), COTS rationale, PDH assessments, and may still record SEE certification considerations. → ch05, ch06, ch07
- **PSAC (plan for software aspects of certification)**: The planning document AMC 20-115D points at: it states how the primary document and each technique supplement apply together, which objectives come from which document, and how planned activities cover them, and it summarises CIA results for modifications (with the SAS as the alternative home). → ch03, ch07
- **SAS (software accomplishment summary)**: The accomplishment-side document where CIA results are summarised after software modification, beside the PSAC. → ch03, ch07
- **SEE (Single Event Effects)**: Hardware susceptibility outside AMC 20-152A's scope, handled through CRI practice with EASA CM-AS-004 Issue 01 named as further guidance (not fetched); no SEE objective appears in the AMC sections this pack carries. → ch05, ch06
- **TQP (tool qualification plan)**: The plan stating which tool-qualification objectives are affected when a qualified tool uses technique-supplement methods, for tool qualification levels 1 through 4. Tool qualification itself points at ED-12C/DO-178C section 12.2 and ED-215/DO-330 (name only, not reproduced). → ch04, ch07
