# Chapter: Implement DMSMS resolutions

## Core Idea

Implement funds and fields the preferred resolution, then closes the case. Section 7 of SD-22, 06-JAN-2026 covers programming and budgeting for resolutions, integration with modification funding, execution issues, and FMS implement notes. Method detail for operations budgeting, resolution budgeting, record-keeping benefits, roadmaps, and provisioning folds in from Appendices H, N, I, K, and O. No budget tables, quad charts, or worksheet cells are reproduced.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Resolution funding

- **Program and budget for resolutions.** Distinct from Prepare operations funding. Tie requests to impact dates from Assess and to the approved option from Analyze.
- **Integrate with modification funding.** Planned mods are natural vehicles for DMSMS fixes when roadmaps and configuration plans already open the system.
- **Other resources.** Working-capital and centralized accounts may help in some commodities; competition for those accounts is real. Appendix N describes approaches without copying its tables.

### Execution

Once funded, the case enters implementation: procurement or redesign work, logistics and technical-manual updates, training changes, and configuration control. DMT roles shift from optioneering to tracking completion and residual risk.

## Key Concepts

- **Impact date drives the budget calendar.** Late funding converts an affordable plan into a readiness hit.
- **Modification windows.** Sync resolutions with roadmap-driven mods to avoid double-opening the system.
- **FMS implement notes.** Partner cases, shared buys, and notification closeout belong in execution, not only in earlier steps.
- **Record keeping.** Good case data improves the next estimate and the next leadership brief (Appendix I).
- **Provisioning hygiene.** Wrong user lists understate demand for life-of-need buys and leave systems unsupported after a fix.

## Mental Models

- **Two money pipes.** Operations keep the watch running; resolution dollars buy the fix. Starve either pipe and the process fails.
- **Roadmap as calendar.** Appendix K roadmaps are the shared clock among DMSMS, technology insertion, and mods.
- **Close the loop.** Implementation is not done until logistics, training, and provisioning reflect the new state.

## Key Takeaways

- Build resolution budget packages from Assess timing and Analyze selection.
- Prefer modification and roadmap alignment when it reduces reopen cost.
- Track execution through DMT roles until logistics and provisioning catch up.
- Use Appendices H, N, I, K, and O as method references; copy no cells.
- Apply FMS implement practice whenever partner configurations were in the case.
