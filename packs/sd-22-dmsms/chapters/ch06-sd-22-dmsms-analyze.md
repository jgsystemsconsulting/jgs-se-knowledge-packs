# Chapter: Analyze resolution determination

## Core Idea

Analyze turns an Assessed need into a preferred resolution. Section 6 of SD-22, 06-JAN-2026 covers cost elements, the option set, viability screening, system security engineering considerations, BCA or AoA practice, leadership approval, and handoff into Implement. Appendix M summarizes Department of Commerce cost-survey results at method level. No survey cells are copied.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Cost elements and option families

Resolution cost is more than unit price. Elements include engineering, qualification, logistics updates, support equipment, training, and provisioning data changes. Options fall into broad families such as life-of-need buys, substitutes or alternates, redesign, and technology refreshment. Exact option tables stay in the source; this pack keeps the family names and decision logic.

### Viability to selection flow

1. List candidate resolutions and their cost elements.
2. Drop options that fail technical, schedule, security, or data-rights screens.
3. Weigh remaining options with BCA or AoA discipline matched to stakes.
4. Fold in system security engineering when foreign-adversary or assurance risk is in play (section 6.3.3). This is a consideration inside Analyze, not a separate chapter or cluster move.
5. Seek initial program-office leadership approval.
6. Prepare the Implement handoff (funding package, configuration hooks, FMS notes).

### FMS analyze notes

Partner equipment may share or split resolutions. Technical-data access and case structure can change which option is viable for the foreign partner versus the DoD fleet.

## Key Concepts

- **BCA vs AoA.** Use lighter analysis when low-cost, low-risk options dominate. Use fuller business-case work when net-present-value, residual risk, and multi-year funding differ sharply across options.
- **Security engineering consideration.** Specialty engineering input can eliminate or reshape an otherwise cheap substitute.
- **Cost survey context.** Appendix M aggregates historical resolution costs by type and commodity. Use it as orientation, not as a paste-in rate card.
- **Approval gate.** Analyze ends with leadership acceptance of the preferred path, not with an unfunded preference.

## Mental Models

- **Cheap now vs cheap life-cycle.** A life-of-need buy can beat repeated bridge buys; a redesign can beat endless last-time buys when demand is long.
- **Security is a screen, not a slogan.** If a resolution opens an assurance hole, it is not viable even if the unit cost is low.
- **Shared vs split fixes.** FMS demand can fund a better shared buy, or force a DoD-only path when data rights block partner use.

## Key Takeaways

- Cost the full element set, not just hardware unit price.
- Screen for viability, including system security engineering when section 6 conditions apply.
- Match BCA or AoA depth to decision stakes.
- Summarize cost-survey lessons; never copy Appendix M cells.
- Leave Analyze with an approved preferred resolution ready for Implement funding.
