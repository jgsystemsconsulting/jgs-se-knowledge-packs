# SD-22 DMSMS Cheatsheet (06-JAN-2026)

Quick rules for SD-22, 06-JAN-2026 (cover dated December 2025). Synthesized notes. No matrix, checklist, or budget cells.

## Five steps (no Strategize overlay)

| Step | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| Prepare | Who owns it, what is the plan, is operations money and FMS communication in place? |
| Identify | What is failing or about to fail on supply versus demand? |
| Assess | Do we act, when, and at what assembly level? |
| Analyze | Which resolution wins on cost, risk, schedule, and security screens? |
| Implement | Is the fix funded, fielded, and closed in logistics and provisioning? |

## Start here

- No DMT or DMP? Start at Prepare (ch03).
- Dirty or missing BOM? Identify plus Appendix J method (ch04).
- PDN or forecast hit just arrived? Assess whether/when/level (ch05).
- Need chosen but unfunded? Implement budgeting (ch07), not another Analyze loop.
- Partner configuration involved? Check FMS notes in Prepare, Identify, Analyze, Implement.

## State versus event

- **Obsolete with enough stock** may be watch-only.
- **Current item, failed source** can still be a DMSMS issue.
- Same five-step process either way (ch08).

## Resolution families (names only)

Life-of-need buy, substitute or alternate, redesign, technology refreshment. Cost full elements (engineering, quals, logistics updates, training, provisioning), not unit price alone.

## Funding split

- **Operations** keep Prepare and Identify running (Appendix H method).
- **Resolutions** buy the fix (Appendix N method).
- Starve either pipe and readiness pays later.

## Hard limits

Does NOT teach general systems engineering, parts-management standards, or contracting law in depth, and does not reproduce DoD policy text or the source's tables and figures.
