# Patterns — DAFMAN 63-119 (MOTRC)

## 1. Early Template Applicability Sweep
- **When:** Program inception / first ITT meetings.
- **How:** Review all Attachments 2–32 for applicability; park only clear non-applies; schedule long-lead items first; record strategy in TEMP Part III.
- **Trade-offs:** Upfront workshop time vs late discovery of unresourced OT enablers.

## 2. Independent Certification Official
- **When:** MDAP, ACAT I/II, or OSD oversight programs.
- **How:** Designate Official in TEMP (SAF/AQ or PEO path); forbid PM self-certification; keep Official advised by ITT throughout.
- **Trade-offs:** Extra leadership bandwidth vs compromised independence.

## 3. Rising-Cadence Review Series
- **When:** Any program approaching OA or dedicated OT.
- **How:** IMS-linked pre-cert reviews with increasing frequency; candid status narratives; exit criteria for each shortfall; get-well plans owned by named OPRs.
- **Trade-offs:** Meeting load vs end-of-DT surprise slips.

## 4. Integrated-Event OT Credit Design
- **When:** Maximizing integrated testing to reduce dedicated OT scope.
- **How:** Pre-brief DOT&E/OTO on event characterization, traceability to OT needs, representativeness, scoring controls, and configuration/data management.
- **Trade-offs:** Planning rigor vs re-running dedicated events later.

## 5. Cyber-on-Every-Wave
- **When:** Any networked or software-rich system.
- **How:** MRAP-C/MBCRA early; cyber requirements testable; cyber activities inside DT and OT; revisit A9/A12/A19 when architecture/threat shifts.
- **Trade-offs:** Continuous cyber cost vs late untestable attack-surface surprises.

## 6. Memo as Candor Contract
- **When:** Final certification before dedicated OT (and per increment/release).
- **How:** Include structure, not-ready items, limitations/deferrals, disagreements; submit ≥15 days out; obtain OTO acknowledge; escalate non-concur cleanly.
- **Trade-offs:** Political discomfort vs untraceable OT start conditions.

## 7. Decertify Cleanly, Recertify Fully
- **When:** Stop-test for poor performance or chronic pause pattern.
- **How:** Official decertifies on OTO/PM written recommendation; return to DT; revisit templates; recertify by full memo process before OT resume.
- **Trade-offs:** Schedule hit vs wasted OT on an unready system.

## 8. Supplier Data Rights Up Front
- **When:** COTS/NDI or heavy contractor-test programs.
- **How:** Contract for early legacy/contractor test data, support, and configuration visibility; track via contractor-testing and contractor-support templates.
- **Trade-offs:** Negotiation effort at award vs blind spots at certification.
