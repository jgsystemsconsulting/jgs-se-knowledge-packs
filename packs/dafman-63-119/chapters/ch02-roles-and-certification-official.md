# Chapter 2: Roles, Responsibilities, and the Certification Official

## Core Idea
MOTRC is a cross-community process. Acquisition, requirements, developers, IT management, T&E, operations, and sustainment all contribute data and candid feedback. The Mission-Oriented Test Certification Official (successor title to the former OT&E Certification Official) owns the readiness certification decision—never the PM for MDAP/ACAT I–II or OSD-oversight programs.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Certification Official designation**: Documented in the TEMP/test strategy; sets broad scope for certifying readiness to begin each test phase and certifies readiness (T-2). Must not be the program’s own PM for MDAP, ACAT I/II, or OSD oversight (T-1).
- **SAF/AQ**: Air Force SAE; MDA and Certification Official for ACAT I MDAP unless delegated to the PEO; may appeal OTO non-concurrence on certification memos.
- **AF/TE**: Senior test executive for USAF and USSF; may appeal OTO non-concurrence.
- **PEO**: Certification Official for ACAT II or OSD-oversight cases (as assigned).
- **ITT co-chair model**: CDT/TM (government DT lead) co-chairs the Integrated Test Team with the lead OTO; ITT advises the Certification Official and may stand up a MOTRC subgroup.

## Key Concepts
- **PM duties (high-signal)**: Early POC for organizing certification (often CDT/TM) before first ITT (T-3); brief process and templates; gather inputs, schedule reviews, negotiate issues, draft memo; underpin development with robust SE (e.g., digital engineering); ensure end-to-end functionality/performance/operability/safety in DT before OT readiness cert; verify viable/stable capability and operationally relevant test environments; safety release before hazardous events; document certification strategy in TEMP Part III; pull in extra stakeholders; write COTS/NDI contracts for early legacy test-data access.
- **CDT / TM**: Government T&E professional coordinating DT including contractor testing; objective judgments on DT results; reports to PM; co-chairs ITT.
- **AFOTEC**: USAF OTA (and USSF OTA until USSF OTA stands up) (T-1). When designated OTO: ITT co-chair; early involvement; tailored, continuous feedback; integrated/combined test; support PEO review with OT data status, significant cyber risk-assessment findings (e.g., MBCRA/MRAP-C), remaining OT needs, OT completion risks; mobilize dedicated-OT resources.
- **MAJCOM / Space Force OTO**: Same certification functions as AFOTEC when they own OT. Template acronym **OTO** means AFOTEC, MAJCOM OTO, or USSF OTO as applicable.
- **Lead operating command**: Requirements currency (AFI 10-601 / AF/A5R guidebooks), SME support, test resources (T-2).
- **LDTO**: Lead DT integrator separate from the program office; plans/conducts/reports DT; oversees contractor DT results with CDT; feeds certification with analysis and recommendations (T-1).
- **Participating test orgs**: Support integrated/OT execution and feed PM certification needs (T-1).
- **HQ DAF / OSD staff**: Monitor effectiveness; update templates as policy moves; DD(DTE&A) and DOT&E may observe.
- **ITT**: Assign key members to certification reviews (T-1); build certification into overarching T&E strategy at inception (T-1); invite interoperability players (e.g., AFSIT, JITC) when net-ready attributes matter; may reassign OPRs.

## Mental Models
- **Independence of the Certification Official** protects OT integrity on high-visibility programs.
- **POC + ITT co-chairs** are the operating system of the process; the Official is the decision apex.
- **OTO is a family name** — resolve which organization fills it before assigning template OPRs.

## Anti-patterns
- PM self-certifying an oversight/MDAP program.
- Standing up the certification POC after TEMP freeze / late DT.
- Excluding lead operating command until the final briefing.
- Confusing LDTO (DT provider) with the Certification Official.

## Key Takeaways
1. Certification Official ≠ PM for MDAP/ACAT I–II/OSD oversight.
2. PM owns execution logistics; CDT/TM + OTO co-chair the ITT.
3. AFOTEC/MAJCOM/USSF OTOs share the OTO role pattern.
4. LDTO and participating orgs supply independent DT/OT evidence into the package.
