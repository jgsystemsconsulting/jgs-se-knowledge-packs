# Chapter 6: Integration, ITT, and Cyber Resiliency in Certification

## Core Idea
Integration is both organizational (Integrated Test Team) and technical (combined/integrated test events, interoperability, cyber-contested environments). MOTRC succeeds when the ITT builds certification into the T&E strategy at inception and keeps OT credit, cyber resiliency, and multi-org OPRs synchronized.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **ITT as certification engine**: Co-chaired by CDT/TM and lead OTO; assigns roles; may create a MOTRC subgroup for detailed work while ITT stays executive. Full authority to add/change OPRs. Invites interoperability specialists (e.g., AFSIT, JITC) when net-ready attributes apply.
- **ITT standup template**: Attachment 6 (ITT standup & charter) is an early long-lead item—charter clarity prevents late role fights.
- **Integrated test planning templates**: Attachments 10 (TEMP), 11 (integrated test planning), 22 (OT&E plan) lock strategy, data, and entrance/exit criteria that MOTRC will later judge.
- **Cyber resiliency as continuous integration**: Threat-informed requirements, measurable cyber requirements, iterative cyber T&E in DT and OT, MRAP-C/MBCRA prioritization, and TEMP/CRD resourcing (T-1 / T-3 hooks in ch03).

## Key Concepts
- **Supplier/industry interface**: Contractor testing (A13) and contractor support (A30) templates force explicit industry deliverables—data, support, and configuration discipline—into the readiness story (Supplier/Industry cluster vocabulary).
- **Interoperability external articles**: Resource templates call out availability of interoperable external systems/subsystems as OT enablers—integration risk is a certification risk.
- **Threat & CONOPS alignment**: Attachments 5 and 7 keep threat/intelligence and CONOPS current so OT environments and cyber emulation stay mission-oriented.
- **Configuration management**: Attachment 18 cross-cuts design and test—configuration surveillance underpins OT credit claims.
- **Live fire / specialized integration**: Where applicable, LFT&E (A16) and related survivability/lethality threads join the matrix rather than living outside MOTRC.
- **Multi-service integration**: Lead-service certification policy rules; DAF still runs its portions and may adapt into partner processes via ITT agreements.

## Mental Models
- **Certification is a team sport with a single Official referee.**
- **If it is not in the TEMP/integrated plan, it will not be certifiable on schedule.**
- **Cyber and interoperability failures are mission failures** — treat them as core MOTRC content.

## Anti-patterns
- ITT that never looks at certification templates until final OT planning.
- Cyber testing bolted on after DT complete.
- Net-ready systems without JITC/AFSIT (or equivalent) engagement.
- Industry data rights discovered only when drafting the certification memo.

## Key Takeaways
1. ITT co-chairs own day-to-day certification orchestration; Official decides.
2. TEMP + integrated test plan + OT plan are the documentary spine of integration.
3. Cyber resiliency and external interoperability are continuous template themes.
4. Supplier/contractor obligations must be contracted early to feed evidence.
