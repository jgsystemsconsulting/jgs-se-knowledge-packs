# Chapter 1: MOTRC Framework and Applicability

## Core Idea
DAFMAN 63-119 (15 April 2021) defines Mission-Oriented Test Readiness Certification (MOTRC) as a continuous, structured risk-evaluation process spanning the full test continuum—from program start through dedicated operational test. It replaces the former single-point Operational Test Readiness Review (OTRR) mindset with early integrated testing and exit-criteria thinking so programs stay on track for effective and suitable performance in real-world operations.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Policy spine**: Implements AFPD 63-1/20-1 and AFI 63-101/20-101 (Integrated Life Cycle Management); fulfills DoDI 5000.89 and AFI 99-103 OTRR-equivalent obligations via continuous certification (T-0 on the mandatory continuous process).
- **Structured risk evaluation**: Cost, schedule, performance, and safety risks are examined with negotiated agreements and candid readiness judgments—not a rubber-stamp gate.
- **Template-backed process**: Thirty-one certification templates (Attachments 2–32) consolidate policy, lessons learned, and best practices; templates are subordinate to formal DoD/DAF directives.
- **Dedicated OT definition**: Independent OT&E phase supporting a deployment or full-rate production (FRP) decision.

## Key Concepts
- **Applicability breadth**: Major acquisition, middle-tier, operational demos/experiments, and any program that will execute operational testing; also COTS/NDI/prototypes when OT&E is planned.
- **What a MOTRC review covers**: Contractor, DT&E, and OT&E results; progress vs KPPs/KSAs/critical technical parameters in the TEMP (or other test strategy); technical-risk closure through DT&E; system certifications; OT entrance/exit criteria (T-2 on PM inclusion of these elements).
- **Defense business systems**: Non-MDAP business systems follow AFMAN 63-144, but programs on the OSD oversight list still use this DAFMAN because T&E planning/execution expectations track AFI 99-103.
- **Increments and agile**: Incremental strategies repeat certification per increment (T-1). Agile/software-pathway programs use applicable templates generally and apply the full process for periodic end-to-end (especially high-volume capacity) tests; see also DoDI 5000.87 and AFI 99-103.
- **Lead-service rule**: Primary source when USAF or USSF is lead. Multi-service: lead-service policy governs; DAF portions should still use this manual. Non-lead DAF members may adapt flow into the other service’s process via the ITT.
- **Rapid/urgent paths**: For prototypes, JCTDs, UON/JEON-like solutions, tailor templates to review capabilities/limitations and initial-deployment readiness; full process may be unnecessary, but a Capabilities and Limitations Report to developer and users is expected (AFI 99-103).
- **DT sufficiency link**: MDAP MDA must assess DT&E sufficiency at MS B and MS C summary reporting (T-0). Service SAE-MDA cases: senior DAF DT&E official (AF/TE) conducts assessments. MS B looks at TEMP DT plans, schedule, resources, risk mitigation, production-entry DT criteria; MS C looks at completed DT, remaining plans/resources, risk mitigation, and readiness for dedicated OT.
- **OT adequacy**: DOT&E reviews OT plan adequacy (10 USC § 139). OTOs collaborate early; adequacy is required before dedicated OT starts and is harder when maximizing integrated testing for OT credit.

## Mental Models
- **Continuous certification beats a single OTRR event** — readiness is earned across the life cycle.
- **Templates are checklists under policy, not a substitute for policy.**
- **OT credit from integrated events must be engineered** (traceability, operational relevance, scoring controls, configuration surveillance).

## Anti-patterns
- Treating MOTRC as a last-minute briefing before OT start.
- Using templates as a yes/no form without status narratives.
- Skipping DT sufficiency / OT adequacy threads because 'we have a TEMP.'
- Applying the full MDAP template set uncritically to a rapid prototype without tailoring.

## Key Takeaways
1. MOTRC = continuous mission-oriented readiness certification replacing single-point OTRR focus.
2. Mandatory through testing to operational acceptance; templates A2–A32 support but do not replace DoD/DAF policy.
3. Scope covers major/MTA/demo/COTS/NDI/oversight business systems and repeats per increment.
4. DT sufficiency (MS B/C) and DOT&E OT adequacy are companion gates to certification quality.
