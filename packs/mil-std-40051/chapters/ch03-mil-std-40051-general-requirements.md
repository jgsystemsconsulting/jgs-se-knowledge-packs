# Chapter: General requirements, IETM functionality, and work packages

## Core Idea

Section 4 states the technical content rules and the mandatory style and format rules for technical publications and their revisions. It covers operation and maintenance through sustainment, including DMWRs and NMWRs, and it frames how electronic and page-based outputs share structure.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Publication family under one spine

General requirements address aircraft maintenance products, PMD/PMS/PMI products, parts information (including RPSTL), troubleshooting manuals, destruction manuals, BDAR, LOs, PMCs, software manuals, and general maintenance manuals. One spine, many TM types.

### Selective application and tailoring

Not every requirement fits every publication. The materiel developer selectively applies and tailors content using Appendix A. Phrases such as "unless specified otherwise by the MATDEV," "as specified by the MATDEV," and "when specified by the MATDEV" mark decision points. Service prefixes further narrow some guidance.

### Structure, IETM behavior, and work packages

- **Linear and non-linear data.** IETM rules distinguish linear presentation from non-linear navigation when the output is interactive.
- **Modules and volumes.** Publications partition into modules; stand-alone parts information products follow the same modular idea.
- **Work packages (WPs).** WPs divide narrative or task-oriented information into independently developed units. Each WP gets a unique identification number and a stable title pattern. Sequence numbers keep order inside a module.
- **XML/DTD orientation.** Authoring assumes structured source that can render to ETM or IETM without rewriting the technical content model.

### Style, warnings, and change hooks

Section 4 also carries style and format obligations, warning/caution/note presentation, and the hooks later detailed for change packages and revisions. Those hooks feed ch08; the structural rules stay here.

## Key Concepts

- **Single content model, dual output.** Write once against the standard; render as page-based or interactive as the contract requires.
- **WP independence.** A WP should stand as a usable unit for training and field citation, not only as a pagination convenience.
- **MATDEV ownership of tailoring.** Authors do not silently drop requirements; they record tailoring through the content-selection path.
- **Safety information.** Warnings, cautions, and notes have formatting and grouping rules for both ETM and header presentations. HAZMAT presentation follows the same safety layer.

## Mental Models

- **Structure before paint.** Settle modules, WPs, and IDs before debating fonts and plate layout.
- **Tailor through the matrix, not the margin.** Selective application is an Appendix A act, not an ad-hoc delete.
- **IETM is not a different standard.** Functionality requirements live inside this volume.

## Key Takeaways

- Section 4 is the shared spine for content, style, and format.
- Work packages are the primary modular unit for narrative and task data.
- IETM functionality and ETM formatting are in-scope here.
- MATDEV tailoring is explicit and matrix-backed.
- Do not copy figure plates or sample WP images into the pack.
