# Chapter 1: Scope, TM Family, and Applicability

## Core Idea
MIL-STD-40051-2C (15 December 2015) is the DoD standard practice for preparing digital technical information for **page-based technical manuals (TMs)**. It sets technical content, style, and format requirements for operator and maintenance manuals and related equipment publications supporting Army and USMC weapon systems (and available DoD-wide), covering maintenance through overhaul/depot including DMWRs and NMWRs.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Page-based TM = ETM synonym here** — for this standard, electronic technical manual (ETM) means page-based TM (not the interactive IETM path in 40051-1).
- **Two-part family** — MIL-STD-40051-1 (IETM) and MIL-STD-40051-2 (page-based); this pack is the **-2C** slice only.
- **Publication types in scope** — operator/maintenance TMs, DMWR/NMWR, PMD/PMS/PMI, destruction manuals, BDAR, PMC, lubrication orders, ammunition DMWRs, software manuals, general maintenance manuals.
- **Audience frame** — Training & Documentation production plus Ops & Maintenance users who depend on comprehensible, correctly framed procedures.

## Key Concepts
- **Applicability** — major weapon systems and related systems, subsystems, assemblies, SRUs/LRUs; operation (except aviation per foreword caveats) and maintenance through depot.
- **Acquiring activity role** — selects content via matrices, tailors with documented decisions, and owns final TM requirements on contract.
- **Comprehensibility** — completeness with which the intended user can understand and apply the information—design driver for reading grade, graphics, and procedure structure.
- **Work package (WP) paradigm** — modular units of information (tasks, supporting info) that assemble into chapters/manuals.
- **Maintenance levels/classes** — content framed to the authorized level (operator through sustainment/depot).
- **Distribution Statement A on the standard itself** — cover states approved for public release; distribution unlimited (TM instances still carry their own distro/export notices).

## Mental Models
- **Standard vs instance** — 40051-2C governs how to build TMs; each TM still gets its own title page notices, effectivity, and classification/CUI markings.
- **Page-based is a delivery choice with rules** — not 'PDF whatever'; front matter, WP structure, and change marks are normative.
- **Training & documentation product** — TMs are formal training/ops artifacts, not informal desk guides.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating 40051-2 rules as optional style suggestions.
- Building IETM behaviors under a -2C page-based contract without -1 authority.
- Omitting DMWR/NMWR/BDAR paths when the acquisition needs them.
- Ignoring intended maintenance level when writing procedures.

## Key Takeaways
1. 40051-2C = page-based TM digital technical information standard (15 Dec 2015).
2. Covers a wide TM/DMWR/NMWR/checklist family through depot.
3. Work packages and content matrices are the selection/production spine.
4. Pack targets Training & Documentation and Ops & Maintenance clusters.
