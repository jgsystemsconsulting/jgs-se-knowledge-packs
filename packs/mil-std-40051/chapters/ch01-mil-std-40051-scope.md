# Chapter: Scope and applicability for technical manuals

## Core Idea

MIL-STD-40051E w/Change 1, 30 July 2025 is one volume. It sets technical content, style, and format rules for Technical Manuals (TMs) and related equipment publications that support major weapon systems and their systems, subsystems, equipment, assemblies, components, Shop Replaceable Units (SRUs), and Line Replaceable Units (LRUs).

The standard applies whether the delivered product is page-based or electronic. The terms IETM and ETM name output-specific rules when needed. When the text does not limit a rule to one output, the rule applies to both.

## Frameworks Introduced

### One-volume consolidation

The foreword states this edition supersedes both the prior IETM part and the prior page-based part. Cover text supersedes the base E edition dated 25 March 2025. The pack identity is the consolidated E Change 1 pin, not a canceled slice.

### Publication types in scope

In scope: operator and maintenance TMs; depot and national maintenance work products (DMWR, NMWR); preventive daily, services, and phased inspection products (PMD, PMS, PMI); destruction, BDAR, PMC, and LO products; ammunition DMWRs; software manuals; general maintenance manuals; hand receipts; ammunition data sheets; and technical bulletins. Coverage runs through depot sustainment levels.

### Service-limited paragraphs

Some paragraphs carry a service prefix: (A) Army, (N) Navy, (MC) Marine Corps, (F) Air Force, (SF) Space Force. Unprefixed text applies to all. MC-prefixed text is Marine Corps-only unless a multi-service rule says otherwise.

## Key Concepts

- **Approved use.** Foreword approves the standard for Army and Marine Corps use and makes it available to all DoD departments and agencies.
- **Approach to this revision.** Updates are pervasive. Change bars and a revision summary are not used. Users should treat the edition as essentially new.
- **Materiel developer decisions.** Limited-applicability and tailoring choices sit with the materiel developer (MATDEV) and the acquiring activity, not with this pack.
- **Single viewer note.** The scope section discusses a single viewer approach for IETMs (IADS). That is a delivery constraint, not a second standard.

## Mental Models

- **Output is not identity.** Page-based and electronic are delivery modes of the same standard practice.
- **Prefix before inventing scope.** Service prefixes narrow a paragraph; absence of a prefix widens it.
- **Consolidation ends the split-family story.** Do not route IETM work to a separate canceled part number.

## Key Takeaways

- Pin is MIL-STD-40051E w/Change 1, 30 July 2025.
- One volume covers page-based and electronic manuals.
- TM family includes operator, maintenance, depot products, BDAR, PMC, LO, software, hand receipts, data sheets, and TBs.
- Service prefixes bound some paragraphs.
- This pack synthesizes rules; it does not copy matrices or plates.
