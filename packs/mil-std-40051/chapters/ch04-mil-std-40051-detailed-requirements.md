# Chapter: Detailed requirements method

## Core Idea

Section 5 turns the general spine into detailed preparation rules for TM front matter, body structure, notices, and related publication controls. The method is requirement-driven and structured-data aware. This pack describes the method. It does not copy matrix cells, sample pages, or figure plates.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Front-matter control set

Detailed requirements cover identification information, security classification handling, multi-service publication numbering, end-item coding, supersession notices, general-purpose notices, publication date, warning summary, list of effective pages/WPs for ETMs, and related title-block fields. Each field has a place in the structured source and a rendering rule for the chosen output.

### Body and control artifacts

- **Warning summary.** Required when the TM contains warnings; prepared as its own controlled element.
- **Change-related front artifacts.** Change transmittal material for ETMs and list-of-effective-pages/WPs logic keep the field copy aligned with the change package.
- **Notices.** Supersession and other notices sit on controlled pages so users see edition state without reading the whole book.

### How detailed requirements relate to appendices

Section 5 states the common detailed method. Appendices specialize content by TM type (operator, troubleshooting, maintenance, parts, supporting information, and special products). Authors satisfy section 5 first, then fill the appendix-driven content that the matrix selected.

## Key Concepts

- **Identification before narrative.** Publication number, date, classification, and supersession state must be correct before procedure text is trusted.
- **Multi-service numbering.** Joint manuals follow the multi-service numbering rule rather than a single-service habit.
- **End-item code.** Include the end-item code when available; mark N/A when the field does not apply.
- **Effectivity visibility.** Lists of effective pages or WPs, plus change markings, are how users know which content is live.
- **No plate substitution.** Sample layouts in the source are illustrations of the method, not content to OCR into this pack.

## Mental Models

- **Method over specimen.** Learn the field set and the decision rules; do not memorize a scanned sample page.
- **Common core, typed limbs.** Section 5 is the core; appendices are typed limbs selected by Appendix A.
- **Structured source is the master.** Rendered page image is a view, not the authority.

## Key Takeaways

- Section 5 defines the detailed preparation method for shared TM controls.
- Front-matter fields and notices are first-class requirements.
- Appendices add type-specific content on top of this method.
- Matrices and plates stay in the source; this pack keeps the method only.
- ch06 and ch07 apply the method to concrete TM families.
