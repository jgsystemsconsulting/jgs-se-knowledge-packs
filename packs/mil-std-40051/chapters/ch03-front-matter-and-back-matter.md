# Chapter 3: Front Matter and Back Matter Requirements

## Core Idea
Section 5 detailed requirements prescribe the front-matter stack (cover through how-to-use) and back-matter closures. These pages carry identity, distribution, warnings, and navigation—failure here makes an otherwise good procedure set unreleasable or unsafe to use.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Front cover** — title, TM number, authenticity markings, distribution statement, export control warning, destruction notice as required, and related cover metadata.
- **Warning summary** — consolidated hazard overview when required; not a substitute for in-procedure warnings.
- **Title page / title block** — formal identity, dates of issue for original and changes, supersession, and related title data.
- **Table of contents** — lists structured content with page numbers; warning summary listing rules apply.
- **How to use this manual / general information** — orientation WPs that teach documentation use and equipment description/theory as selected.
- **Back cover and reporting errors** — back matter includes improvement reporting paths (e.g., DA Form 2028 patterns) and cover closures; multi-volume rules repeat identity elements.

## Key Concepts
- **Distribution statement `<dist>`** — required cover/title elements per DoDI 5230.24 practice as implemented in the standard's element list.
- **Export control warning and destruction notice** — when applicable, same visibility as distribution on cover/title families.
- **Authentication / promulgation** — signature or authenticating block per service practice called out in the TM type.
- **Change dates on title page** — original plus change issue dates keep users on the right amendment state.
- **Multi-volume front matter** — each volume carries prescribed repeated identity elements.
- **DMWR/NMWR title variants** — depot/national maintenance work requirements use specialized title/availability statements.

## Mental Models
- **Cover is a compliance surface** — distro/export/destruction are legal/release controls, not decoration.
- **Warning summary ≠ procedure warnings** — both layers required when hazards exist.
- **Title page is the effectivity billboard** — dates and supersession prevent mixed-change floors.

## Anti-patterns
- Shipping a TM PDF with a decorative cover missing DIST/export blocks when required.
- TOC that omits WP-level entries users need to find tasks.
- No error-reporting path in back matter.
- Stale supersession lines after a change package.

## Key Takeaways
1. Front matter encodes identity, release controls, warnings, and navigation.
2. Distribution/export/destruction elements are first-class cover/title content.
3. Title-page change dates and TOC accuracy are configuration signals.
4. Back matter closes the loop with improvement reporting and covers.
