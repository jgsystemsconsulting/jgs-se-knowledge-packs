# Chapter 7: Content Selection Matrices and Tailoring

## Core Idea
You do not deliver every possible WP. Appendix A (and related matrices) plus section 6 notes drive **content selection matrices** and tailoring so each TM matches equipment, maintenance level, and acquiring-activity decisions—critical for Training & Documentation scope control and Ops & Maintenance relevance.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Content selection matrix** — formal checklist of chapters/WPs/functions to include; filled out as a TM planning artifact.
- **Tailoring authority** — acquiring activity documents tailoring; contractors do not silently drop mandatory safety/identity content.
- **Appendix-driven functional requirements** — appendices B–N interpret technical content structures for operator, maintenance, RPSTL, PM, DMWR, software, destruction, BDAR, etc.
- **Maintenance level filtering** — operator-only vs maintainer vs depot content sets differ by matrix rows.
- **Aviation and special exclusions** — foreword/scope caveats (e.g., aviation operation exceptions) constrain matrices.
- **Deliverable planning** — matrix + style/trim + schedule = TM basis of estimate for documentation programs.

## Key Concepts
- **Mandatory vs optional rows** — safety, identity, and core operation/maintenance tasks vs situation-dependent WPs.
- **RPSTL inclusion** — parts/tools lists selected when authorized; introduction WP patterns apply.
- **PM/PMS/PMI/PMC/LO** — preventive maintenance products selected per fleet policy.
- **Software TM selection** — include when software is user-facing or maintainer-loaded per scope.
- **Destruction/BDAR** — selected for applicable combat equipment sets.
- **Multi-manual programs** — split operator vs maintenance vs DMWR books via coordinated matrices.

## Mental Models
- **Matrix before writing** — prevents orphan chapters and missing authorized tasks.
- **Tailoring is explicit** — if it is not on the matrix or a written tailoring decision, it is uncontrolled.
- **Documentation scope = training scope** — what you select is what schools can teach from the official TM.

## Anti-patterns
- Writing the whole manual then deleting sections ad hoc under schedule pressure.
- Copying another platform's matrix without equipment-specific review.
- Dropping warning summary or distro cover elements while 'tailoring'.
- No matrix under configuration control with the TM baseline.

## Key Takeaways
1. Content selection matrices are the official include/exclude mechanism.
2. Appendices supply functional patterns behind matrix rows.
3. Tailoring is documented acquiring-activity work.
4. Matrix control ties Training & Documentation delivery to Ops needs.
