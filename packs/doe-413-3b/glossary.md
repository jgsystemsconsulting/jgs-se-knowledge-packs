# Glossary — DOE Capital Asset Project Management Order (413.3)

- **Acquisition strategy** — Planned approach for contracting and delivering the capital asset aligned to risk and CD timing. → ch03
- **AoA (Analysis of Alternatives)** — Structured comparison supporting alternative selection (typically toward CD-1). → ch02, ch03
- **CD (Critical Decision)** — Formal authorization gate (CD-0 through CD-4) in the capital asset life cycle. → ch02
- **CRD (Contractor Requirements Document)** — Attachment flowing Order requirements into contracts. → ch01, ch06
- **EVMS** — Earned Value Management System used for integrated cost/schedule performance measurement after CD-2 when required. → ch04
- **FPD (Federal Project Director)** — Federal lead responsible for project direction and outcomes. → ch03, ch06
- **IPT (Integrated Project Team)** — Cross-functional team executing and managing the project. → ch03
- **PARS** — Project Assessment and Reporting System for Department progress/documentation reporting. → ch01, ch04
- **PB (Performance Baseline)** — Approved scope, cost, and schedule commitment established at CD-2. → ch02, ch04
- **PEP (Project Execution Plan)** — Plan describing how the project will be managed and controlled. → ch03
- **PMSO** — Project Management Support Office supporting line organizations. → ch01, ch06
- **PSO** — Program Secretarial Officer (and NNSA analogs) in the approval/line chain. → ch01, ch02
- **TPC (Total Project Cost)** — Total cost figure used for thresholds and authorities. → ch01, ch04
- **Value engineering (VE)** — Function-oriented cost/performance improvement practice aligned to OMB A-131. → ch01, ch05
