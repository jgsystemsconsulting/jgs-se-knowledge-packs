# Chapter 1: Purpose, Applicability, and Governance

## Core Idea
DOE’s capital-asset project management Order directs risk-informed acquisition of capital assets so projects deliver within the original performance baseline (cost and schedule) and meet mission, safeguards/security, and ES&H expectations unless a directed change intervenes. It implements OMB capital-programming and internal-control expectations and defines who must comply, who is exempt or equivalent, and how cancellations interact with existing contracts.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Capital-asset acquisition Order**: Mandatory program/project management direction for covered DOE/NNSA capital asset projects.
- **Performance baseline (PB) goal**: Deliver within original PB cost/schedule with full mission capability, absent directed change.
- **OMB alignment**: Circular A-11 / Capital Programming Guide, A-123 enterprise risk/internal control, A-131 value engineering.
- **Applicability model**: Departmental elements, contractor CRD flow-down, equivalencies/exemptions/delegations.
- **Cancellation continuity**: Newer Order cancels prior 413.3B Chg 7 text; contract CRDs persist until modified.
- **Threshold logic**: TPC above minor-construction threshold or a dollar floor (Order text) drives full requirements and PARS reporting; lower-value work still sees tailored principles.

## Key Concepts
- **Capital asset project**: Acquisition of capital assets under the Order’s scope (not financial assistance/ESPCs as excluded).
- **Line management and Technical Authority touchpoints**: CTA concurrence paths for nuclear-related equivalencies/exemptions.
- **PMSO / PSO roles**: Project Management Support Offices and Program Secretarial Officers in the governance web.
- **NNSA Administrator authorities**: Statutory space recognized without voiding Order duties for NNSA personnel/contractors.
- **Tailoring vs exemption**: Tailored application of principles differs from formal exemption from specific requirements.
- **Successor document awareness**: Pack built from O 413.3C which cancels O 413.3B Chg 7; concepts continue the 413.3 capital-asset regime.

## Mental Models
- The Order is a *governance and decision* system wrapped around project delivery, not a scheduling manual alone.
- Contracts freeze requirements until changed — canceling a directive is not auto-amending every CRD.
- Thresholds sort *how much* process; principles still touch smaller work.
- Risk-informed does not mean optional for covered projects.

## Anti-patterns
- **Assuming grants/ESPCs are in scope**: Order carves them out.
- **Ignoring CRD lag after cancellation**: Contract text may still cite prior revision until modified.
- **Informal “we’re exempt” without the Order’s exemption path**: Especially nuclear CTA concurrence cases.
- **Treating minor construction as process-free**: Principles still apply in tailored form.

## Key Takeaways
1. Purpose is risk-informed capital-asset delivery to PB with mission and ES&H performance.
2. Applicability covers DOE elements and CRD-bearing contractors for in-scope projects.
3. Equivalencies/exemptions follow formal paths (including CTA for nuclear).
4. OMB capital-programming and internal-control expectations are design drivers.
5. Cancellation of prior 413.3B Chg 7 does not by itself rewrite contracts.
6. Thresholds and PARS hooks define the heavyweight regime.
