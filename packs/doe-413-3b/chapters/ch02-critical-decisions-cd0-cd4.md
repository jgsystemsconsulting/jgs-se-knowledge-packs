# Chapter 2: Critical Decisions (CD-0 through CD-4)

## Core Idea
Critical Decisions are the Order’s stage gates for capital asset projects. Each CD has defined intent, approval authority patterns (tied to thresholds and risk), and entry products. Moving from mission need through alternatives, baseline, execution readiness, and transition/closeout is how DOE controls commitment of scope, cost, and schedule.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **CD-0 Approve Mission Need**: Establish that a capability gap justifies a capital asset response.
- **CD-1 Approve Alternative Selection and Cost Range**: Choose the preferred alternative and rough cost range; start shaping acquisition approach.
- **CD-2 Approve Performance Baseline**: Freeze the PB (scope, cost, schedule) that later performance is judged against.
- **CD-3 Approve Start of Construction/Execution**: Authorize major execution/construction commitment with mature design and controls.
- **CD-4 Approve Start of Operations or Project Completion**: Accept transition to operations or declare completion/closeout conditions met.
- **CD approval authority matrix**: Who may approve which CD at which TPC/risk tiers (CE, Under Secretaries, PSOs, etc., per Order appendices).

## Key Concepts
- **Mission Need Statement**: CD-0 cornerstone artifact linking mission gap to project justification.
- **Analysis of Alternatives (AoA)**: Supports CD-1 alternative selection discipline.
- **Performance Baseline**: The committed triple constraint snapshot at CD-2.
- **Design maturity expectations**: Rise across CDs; CD-3 expects execution-ready definition.
- **PARS reporting cadence**: Progress/documentation expectations from CD-0 onward for covered projects.
- **Tailoring across circumstances**: Nuclear, first-of-a-kind, or other special cases may adjust thresholds/pathing under Order rules.

## Mental Models
- CDs are *authorization events*, not status meetings.
- CD-2 is the credibility hinge: after it, EVMS and baseline discipline intensify.
- Skipping CD intent while keeping the label is noncompliance theater.
- Authority follows risk and dollars — know who must sign.

## Anti-patterns
- **CD-2 without a real PB**: Paper baseline that cannot be managed.
- **Construction start mindset before CD-3 conditions**: Premature commitment.
- **Mission need written as a preferred solution**: Collapses AoA.
- **Ignoring lower-threshold tailoring rules**: Either over-processing or under-controlling.

## Key Takeaways
1. CD-0 through CD-4 structure the capital asset life cycle.
2. Each CD has purpose-built products and approval expectations.
3. CD-2 establishes the performance baseline of record.
4. CD-3 authorizes major execution/construction commitment.
5. CD-4 governs operations start or completion/closeout acceptance.
6. Approval authority and thresholds are part of the control system.
