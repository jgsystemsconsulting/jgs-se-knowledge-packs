# Patterns — DOE Capital Asset PM (413.3)

## Pattern: CD-ready evidence pack
- **When**: Approaching any Critical Decision.
- **How**: Assemble mission/alternative/baseline/execution/completion products plus risk posture, review findings, and authority path before the decision meeting.
- **Trade-offs**: Up-front documentation cost; avoids failed CD and rework.

## Pattern: Baseline with explicit change door
- **When**: At/after CD-2.
- **How**: Freeze PB; route all scope/cost/schedule moves through change control; classify directed vs management-caused change.
- **Trade-offs**: Slower informal pivots; protects credibility of EV and PARS.

## Pattern: IPT as decision system
- **When**: From early planning through execution.
- **How**: Charter IPT roles, cadence, risk ownership, and interfaces to FPD/line authority; do not equate attendance with accountability.
- **Trade-offs**: Meeting load; better cross-functional decisions.

## Pattern: Risk-funded contingency
- **When**: Building CD-2 cost and managing execution.
- **How**: Tie contingency to identified risks and update as risks retire or emerge; keep MR ownership rules clear.
- **Trade-offs**: Harder than pad percentages; defensible under review.

## Pattern: Contract mirrors controls
- **When**: Shaping acquisition strategy and CRD.
- **How**: Ensure EVMS, deliverables, change mechanisms, and reporting in the contract match Order expectations for the project class.
- **Trade-offs**: Heavier procurement craft; avoids unenforceable federal expectations.
