# Chapter 5: Distributional Effects and Incidence

## Core Idea
Beyond aggregate net benefits, A-94 directs attention to distributional effects and economic incidence: who gains and who pays, whether weighting is used, and how transfers and burdens land across groups.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Identify affected groups**: Segment stakeholders (income groups, regions, generations, sectors) material to the decision.
- **Weights**: If distributional weights are applied, state them explicitly and justify; default analyses should still report unweighted aggregates and group impacts.
- **Economic incidence**: Trace statutory burdens/benefits to economic actors who ultimately bear or receive them (not only the first payer).

## Key Concepts
- **Aggregate NPV is not the whole story**: Two programs with equal NPV can have very different fairness/political-economy profiles.
- **Transfers again**: Distributional tables often highlight transfers that cancel in aggregate social NPV but matter for incidence.
- **Geographic and sectoral impacts**: Infrastructure and regulatory programs frequently require spatial/sector breakdowns.
- **Intergenerational incidence**: Long-lived capital and environmental effects shift burdens across cohorts—connects to discounting choices.
- **Equity vs efficiency framing**: Keep efficiency (net benefits) metrics distinct from equity displays unless a stated weighting framework combines them.

## Mental Models
- **Ask 'who nets what' after markets adjust, not only 'who is named in the statute.'**
- **Distributional silence is itself a choice—usually a bad one for public decisions.**

## Anti-patterns
- Reporting only national totals for programs with concentrated local costs.
- Applying hidden weights that change rankings without disclosure.
- Confusing budgetary incidence with economic incidence.

## Key Takeaways
1. Identify groups and report distributional impacts alongside aggregate NPV.
2. Make any weights explicit.
3. Trace economic incidence through market adjustments.
4. Keep equity displays transparent relative to efficiency metrics.
