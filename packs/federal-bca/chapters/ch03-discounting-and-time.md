# Chapter 3: Discount Rate Policy and Time

## Core Idea
Because benefits and costs occur over time, A-94 requires present-value comparison using prescribed real and nominal discount-rate policies that differ by analysis type (public investment vs cost-effectiveness, lease-purchase, internal government investment, and asset sales).

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Real vs nominal rates**: Match rate type to whether cash flows are expressed in real (inflation-adjusted) or nominal dollars.
- **Public investment analyses**: Use the circular's prescribed public investment discount-rate approach for social BCA of public investments.
- **Other analysis classes**: Cost-effectiveness, lease-purchase, internal government investment, and asset-sale analyses follow the circular's specific rate guidance for those categories.
- **Appendix rate updates**: OMB publishes periodically updated discount-rate tables/appendices (e.g., Appendix C and related discount-history materials) that analysts must use as current.

## Key Concepts
- **Present value / net present value (NPV)**: Discount dated net benefits to a common time origin for ranking alternatives.
- **Horizon choice**: Long enough to capture material effects; state residual/terminal value treatment explicitly.
- **Rate shopping ban**: Do not pick ad hoc rates to flip rankings; justify any departure only where the circular or controlling law allows.
- **Lease-purchase and capital**: Special discounting guidance exists so financing form is not confused with social cost of capital.
- **Internal government investment / asset sales**: Distinct policy paths so government proprietary-type decisions use appropriate rates.
- **Sensitivity on rates**: Where uncertainty about rates matters, show how rankings change under circular-consistent sensitivity cases (pairs with ch04).

## Mental Models
- **Discounting is not optional seasoning—it is the time-consistency rule that makes alternatives comparable.**
- **Wrong rate family (real vs nominal) is a unit-conversion error.**

## Anti-patterns
- Applying a private WACC as if it were A-94 public investment guidance without basis.
- Discounting nominal flows with a real rate (or the reverse).
- Using outdated appendix rates after OMB updates.

## Key Takeaways
1. Use A-94's prescribed real/nominal rates by analysis class.
2. Keep inflation basis and rate basis aligned.
3. Track OMB appendix updates for current numerical rates.
4. Present NPV with transparent horizon and terminal-value assumptions.
