# Chapter 6: Reporting and Using BCA Results

## Core Idea
A-94's value is realized when analyses are documented clearly enough for OMB, agency leadership, and the public to see assumptions, alternatives, net benefits, uncertainties, and distributional effects—and when results actually inform initiate/renew/expand/contract/stop decisions.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Decision-oriented reporting**: Lead with the decision question, alternatives, NPV/CEA results, and key sensitivities.
- **Reproducibility**: Provide enough data, methods, and sources that a reviewer can reconstruct the result.
- **Consistency with A-11/A-19 packages**: Align tables and assumptions with budget/legislative submission requirements when those paths apply.

## Key Concepts
- **Required narrative pieces**: Baseline, alternatives, time horizon, benefit/cost categories, inflation/discount bases and rates, uncertainty treatment, distributional notes, and limitations.
- **Comparability**: Use consistent units and assumptions across alternatives; highlight any unavoidable inconsistencies.
- **Non-monetized effects**: Present structured qualitative/quantitative non-monetized impacts rather than burying them.
- **Update discipline**: When OMB discount appendices change, refresh living analyses used in multi-year programs.
- **Rescope note (build-time)**: This pack is A-94-only; Army CBA process templates were not available at build (P7-PRE-2 path). For Army-specific CBA step guides, obtain the ASAFM guide separately when accessible.

## Mental Models
- **If leadership cannot see what would flip the recommendation, the report failed.**
- **Transparency is a control against motivated rate and assumption shopping.**

## Anti-patterns
- Delivering a black-box spreadsheet NPV with no baseline story.
- Hiding fatal limitations in footnotes after a glossy executive summary.
- Mixing A-94 social BCA with purely budgetary scorekeeping without labeling.

## Key Takeaways
1. Report decision question, alternatives, NPV/CEA, sensitivities, and incidence clearly.
2. Document rates, inflation basis, and data sources for OMB-grade review.
3. Present non-monetized effects structurally.
4. Keep analyses updated when OMB rate tables change.
