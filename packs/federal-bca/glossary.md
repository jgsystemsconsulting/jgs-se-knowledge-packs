# Glossary — OMB Circular A-94 (2023)

- **Baseline** — State of the world without the policy/program alternative; benefits and costs are measured as differences from it. → ch02
- **Benefit-cost analysis (BCA)** — Comparison of social benefits and costs, typically summarized as discounted net benefits. → ch01, ch02
- **Cost-effectiveness analysis (CEA)** — Comparison of costs for a given effect (or effects per cost) when full monetization of benefits is not used. → ch01
- **Discount rate** — Rate used to convert future benefits/costs to present value; A-94 prescribes real/nominal rates by analysis class. → ch03
- **Distributional effects** — How benefits and costs fall across groups (income, region, sector, generation). → ch05
- **Economic incidence** — Who ultimately bears costs or receives benefits after market adjustments. → ch05
- **Expected value** — Probability-weighted average outcome used when uncertainty is quantified. → ch04
- **Net present value (NPV)** — Present value of benefits minus present value of costs. → ch03, ch06
- **Nominal dollars / rate** — Current-dollar units including inflation; paired with nominal discount rates. → ch02, ch03
- **Opportunity cost** — Value of resources in their next-best use. → ch02
- **Real dollars / rate** — Inflation-adjusted units; paired with real discount rates. → ch02, ch03
- **Sensitivity analysis** — Systematic variation of key assumptions to test result robustness. → ch04
- **Social benefits / social costs** — Effects on society as a whole, not only federal budget outlays. → ch02
- **Transfer** — Redistribution that may cancel in aggregate social NPV but still matters for incidence. → ch02, ch05
