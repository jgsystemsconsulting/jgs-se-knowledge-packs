# Cheatsheet: nasa-de-acquisition (NASA-HDBK-1004A)

Quick path through NASA-HDBK-1004A, approved 2026-10-26. Guidance handbook. Binding force comes only after incorporation into a contract instrument.

## One-pass flow

1. Confirm scope and guidance status (ch01).
2. Frame the Digital Engineering Ecosystem and planning artifacts (ch02).
3. Stress-test with lessons patterns (ch03).
4. Apply guiding principles (ch04).
5. Run guiding questions before any clause draft (ch05).
6. Place language across SOW, DRD, solicitation, and cost volume (ch06).
7. Select and tailor RCL via the matrix method (ch07).
8. Select and tailor DRD templates from the library (ch08).

## Decision shortcuts

- **Missing rights language?** Treat as a future paid re-buy (ch03).
- **Document-only delivery?** Push machine-readable, neutral-format path (ch02, ch06).
- **Unsure which RCL?** Finish section 7 questions first (ch05, ch07).
- **Full DRD suite urge?** Library select, do not shelf-dump (ch08).
- **External standard needed?** Name and version-pin it; do not paste its text.
- **Companion depth?** Practitioner DE detail may sit in NASA-HDBK-1009; this pack does not reproduce it.

## Do not

- Copy RCL rows, matrix cells, DRD fields, or question-table cells into the contract from this pack.
- Put download URLs or host names in pack files.
- Claim the handbook alone binds a contractor.
- Keep superseded-baseline framing as the current pin.
