# Chapter: Digital engineering acquisition overview

## Core Idea

Digital engineering acquisition is how NASA identifies, obtains, controls, exchanges, and preserves the technical data and models needed for lifecycle decisions. It sits inside the broader program and systems-engineering frame set by Agency directives such as NPR 7120.5 and NPR 7123.1. The buy is not "documents if convenient"; the buy is usable, interoperable data and models that can enter NASA repositories and digital threads with minimal translation.

A Digital Engineering Ecosystem connects data, models, tools, repositories, people, and processes across disciplines and phases. Typical capability families include model-based definition, CAD, MBSE, modeling and simulation, and product data and lifecycle management. Acquisition planning decides how those artifacts are created, exchanged, updated, protected, and delivered between NASA and contractor systems.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Data-centric acquisition.** Prefer models and machine-readable data over document-only packages that must be retyped into the digital thread.
- **Federated authoritative sources.** NASA's approach relies on authoritative information sources and structured, machine-interpretable data rather than one monolithic file dump.
- **DE planning linkage.** Capture DE acquisition choices in the program DE Plan (or a short extension of it). Keep those choices aligned to the Project Plan, the SEMP, and the Acquisition Plan that applies.
- **Integrator role.** Because DE is multidisciplinary, programs should consider a DE lead or equivalent integrator for models, data sets, interfaces, and acquisition choices.

## Key Concepts

- **What acquisition emphasizes.** Obtain data and models in usable forms and enable exchange among NASA, contractors, partners, and authoritative repositories.
- **Digital thread feed.** Document-centric content that needs heavy labor to reformat is treated as a risk to the thread, not as a success case.
- **Clear deliverable shape.** Effective implementation needs defined deliverables, data structures, schemas, relationships, and interoperability between NASA and contractor systems.
- **Partnership reach.** Contractual guidance should cover digital tools, methods, and deliverables that support collaboration with domestic and international partners where applicable.

## Mental Models

- **Ecosystem, not tool.** Buying a CAD seat is not the same as buying a DE acquisition framework. The framework is the set of approaches that make data flow across the lifecycle.
- **Plan before clause.** Place acquisition choices in the DE Plan / SEMP / Acquisition Plan stack before pasting language into a solicitation.
- **Minimal translation.** Success looks like data that enters NASA systems without a second reconstruction project.

## Key Takeaways

- DE acquisition is lifecycle data and model procurement, control, and exchange.
- The Digital Engineering Ecosystem is the connective tissue across tools, people, and repositories.
- Planning artifacts should name what is created, exchanged, protected, and delivered.
- A DE lead or integrator role reduces cross-discipline drift.
- Prefer machine-readable, authoritative forms over document-only delivery.
