# Patterns: nasa-de-acquisition (NASA-HDBK-1004A)

Reusable moves from NASA-HDBK-1004A, approved 2026-10-26. Each pattern is pack wording, not clause text.

## Pattern: Select then bind

**When.** Team wants handbook language in a solicitation.

**How.** Answer guiding questions, select RCL and DRD templates, tailor, then incorporate into SOW/PWS, DRD, and cost volume with procurement support.

**Trade-offs.** Slower up front. Prevents unenforceable paste and unpriced data scope.

## Pattern: Three-home data need

**When.** A data deliverable matters to the digital thread.

**How.** Give it a SOW outcome, a DRD shape (contents/format/timing/control), and a cost-volume home.

**Trade-offs.** More artifacts to keep consistent. Avoids delivered-but-unpaid and required-but-undefined outcomes.

## Pattern: Minimum essential with re-compete fuel

**When.** Pressure exists to buy every possible model and file.

**How.** Trace each data call to a lifecycle need and to future competition. Drop the rest.

**Trade-offs.** May defer nice-to-have analytics. Protects cost and keeps later sources viable.

## Pattern: Neutral exchange over native convenience

**When.** Contractor prefers proprietary encodings.

**How.** Require agreed neutral or open exchange forms named in the DRD, plus source-to-destination mapping into NASA systems.

**Trade-offs.** Up-front format work. Avoids lock-in and conversion balloon payments seen in lessons.

## Pattern: Early marks and stewardship

**When.** Large document and model volumes will accumulate.

**How.** Set classification, export, CUI, and records stewardship rules at creation, not at closeout.

**Trade-offs.** Process overhead early. Prevents disposition backlogs and stranded media.

## Pattern: Library DRD shelf

**When.** Appendix B looks mandatory end-to-end.

**How.** Pull only template classes the thread needs; tailor fields; add program-unique DRDs for gaps; legal/procurement review before binding use.

**Trade-offs.** Requires judgment. Beats a fake full suite that no one can afford or enforce.

## Pattern: Rights before closeout

**When.** Heritage or commercial buys under-specify data access.

**How.** Put tailored rights and delivery language in the initial instrument; verify flow-down; name authoritative sources.

**Trade-offs.** Negotiation time at award. Cheaper than end-of-contract repurchase of code and data.

## Pattern: Principles filter RCL

**When.** Matrix selection drifts into maximalism.

**How.** Run FAIR, minimum-essential, and future-competition principles as a reject filter on candidate RCL rows.

**Trade-offs.** Fewer clauses. Higher signal and clearer pricing.
