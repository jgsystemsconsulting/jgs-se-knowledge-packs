# Chapter 4: Uncertainty Characterization and Sensitivity

## Core Idea
Credibility requires explicit uncertainty work in both development and use. Development records how referent-data uncertainty was characterized and what uncertainties are built into the M&S. Use-phase work records uncertainty in inputs and outputs, the processes used, and sensitivity analyses that show what drives results.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Referent-data uncertainty process**: Methods and rationale for characterizing uncertainty in validation referents.
- **Embedded M&S uncertainty record**: Qualitative or quantitative uncertainties incorporated into the model itself.
- **Use-phase uncertainty process**: How input and result uncertainties were characterized for the analysis.
- **Use-phase uncertainty values**: Documented qualitative descriptions or quantitative estimates for inputs and results.
- **Sensitivity analysis record**: Extent and outcomes of sensitivity studies performed with the M&S.
- **Reporting hooks**: Uncertainty estimates and process descriptions are mandatory elements of decision-maker reports.

## Key Concepts
- **Aleatory vs epistemic (practical view)**: Variability vs knowledge gaps; both may appear; label them honestly.
- **Qualitative is allowed when quantitative is not credible**: Still must be recorded with rationale.
- **Sensitivity as navigator**: Shows which inputs/assumptions dominate; guides where pedigree must be strongest.
- **Uncertainty process vs number**: Decision makers need both the estimate and how it was obtained.
- **Linkage to acceptance criteria**: Uncertainty criteria are part of the programmatic acceptance set.
- **Handbook depth**: Comparative uncertainty bounds, capability assessment factors, and analysis guidance.

## Mental Models
- A point estimate without uncertainty is an incomplete M&S result.
- Uncertainty characterization is continuous: referent to model form to inputs to outputs.
- Sensitivity tells you where not to be sloppy.
- Process transparency beats false precision.

## Anti-patterns
- **Error bars invented at briefing time**: No recorded process.
- **Ignoring referent noise while validating**: Contaminates domain claims.
- **Sensitivity only on friendly parameters**: Hides fragile assumptions.
- **Quantitative theater**: Fancy intervals with unjustified distributions.

## Key Takeaways
1. Characterize and record referent-data uncertainty during development.
2. Record uncertainties built into the M&S formulation.
3. In use, record processes and values for input and result uncertainty.
4. Perform and document sensitivity analyses appropriate to the decision.
5. Carry uncertainty estimate and method into decision reporting.
6. Align uncertainty work with pre-set acceptance criteria.
