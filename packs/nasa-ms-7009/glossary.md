# Glossary — NASA M&S Standard (7009B) and Handbook

- **Acceptance criteria (M&S)** — Pre-declared V&V, uncertainty, sensitivity, and assessment-threshold criteria used to judge the M&S. → ch01, ch03
- **Capability assessment** — Structured evaluation of M&S strength across factors such as data pedigree, verification, validation, and uncertainty. → ch02, ch06, ch07
- **Criticality assessment** — Evaluation of M&S influence on a decision versus consequence of decision error. → ch01
- **Domain of validation** — Envelope of conditions where validation claims hold. → ch03, ch05
- **Domain of verification** — Envelope of conditions where verification claims hold. → ch03, ch05
- **Input pedigree** — Provenance and quality story for analysis inputs. → ch05
- **Intended use** — Recorded purpose and decision context that starts the M&S life cycle. → ch01
- **M&S (Models and Simulations)** — Computational or logical representations used to understand or predict RWS behavior. → ch01
- **Permissible uses** — Uses allowed at completion of development given assumptions, limits, and V&V domains. → ch02, ch05
- **Proposed use** — The specific use claimed for a given analysis campaign. → ch05
- **Referent** — Real-world data or knowledge used as the basis for validation comparisons. → ch03, ch04
- **Results assessment** — Structured evaluation of a particular M&S analysis product in use. → ch05, ch06
- **RWS (Real-World System)** — The system or phenomenon the M&S is intended to represent. → ch02
- **Technical Authority (delegated)** — NASA authority partnering with programs on M&S criticality and compliance. → ch01, ch07
- **Uncertainty characterization** — Qualitative or quantitative description of unknowns in referents, model form, inputs, or results. → ch04, ch06
