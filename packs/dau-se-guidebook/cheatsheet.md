# Cheatsheet: DoD Systems Engineering Guidebook (September 2026)

Quick rules for the September 2026 edition. Guidance, not policy.

## Edition spine

1. Introduction and life-cycle framing under AAF and DoDI 5000.88.
2. System-level context; SoS depth moved to updated SoS guide.
3. SE Modernization (new section): DE, MBSE, mission engineering, SWE, MOSA, Agile SE.
4. Engineering resources, enablers, lessons learned, contracting role.
5. Technical reviews and audits (ASR through PCA).
6. SE processes: 6.1 technical management (8) + 6.2 technical (8).
7. Twenty-four design considerations in one section.

## 16 processes

**Technical management:** Technical Planning, Decision Analysis, Technical Assessment, Requirements Management, Risk Management, Configuration Management, Technical Data Management, Interface Management.

**Technical:** Stakeholder Requirements Definition, Requirements Analysis, Architecture Design, Implementation, Integration, Verification, Validation, Transition.

## Review vocabulary

ASR -> SRR -> SFR -> PDR -> CDR -> SVR/FCA -> PRR -> PCA.

Entrance evidence before the meeting. Exit criteria close the event. Agile iterations still feed baseline questions.

## Standing controls

- Keep a living SEP.
- Track TPMs and leading indicators.
- Control baselines, interfaces, and technical data rights.
- Screen all 24 design considerations; deep-dive the drivers.
- Scale rigor to pathway and risk; do not drop safety or security silently.

## Do / Do not

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Tailor to pathway and risk | Treat the book as a mandatory checklist |
