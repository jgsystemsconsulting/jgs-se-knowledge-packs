# Glossary: DoD Systems Engineering Guidebook (September 2026)

Short terms and working definitions synthesized from the September 2026 edition. Not a dump of the source acronym list. Two-column PDF extract of the Acronyms appendix is unreliable; definitions here are rebuilt from body use.

## Acquisition and policy

- **AAF** - Adaptive Acquisition Framework under DoDI 5000.02.
- **AAFDID** - Adaptive Acquisition Framework Document Identification tool for statutory and regulatory document needs.
- **ACAT** - Acquisition Category.
- **DoW** - Department of War (cover and body branding in this edition).
- **DoD / DoDI / DoDD** - Department of Defense instructions and directives still named in policy cites.
- **MDAP** - Major Defense Acquisition Program.
- **OUSW(R&E)** - Office of the Under Secretary of War for Research and Engineering.
- **SE&A** - Systems Engineering and Architecture (office under OUSW(R&E)).
- **SEP** - Systems Engineering Plan.

## Reviews and audits

- **ASR** - Alternative Systems Review.
- **SRR** - System Requirements Review.
- **SFR** - System Functional Review.
- **PDR** - Preliminary Design Review.
- **CDR** - Critical Design Review.
- **SVR** - System Verification Review.
- **FCA** - Functional Configuration Audit.
- **PRR** - Production Readiness Review.
- **PCA** - Physical Configuration Audit.

## Processes and measures

- **CM** - Configuration Management.
- **IMP** - Integrated Master Plan.
- **IMS** - Integrated Master Schedule.
- **TPM** - Technical Performance Measure.
- **WBS** - Work Breakdown Structure.
- **ICWG** - Interface Control Working Group.
- **ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288** - International SE process standard used as a mapping reference.

## Modernization and architecture

- **DE** - Digital Engineering.
- **MBSE** - Model-Based Systems Engineering.
- **MOSA** - Modular Open Systems Approach.
- **CI/CD** - Continuous Integration / Continuous Delivery.
- **DevSecOps** - Development, Security, and Operations practices.
- **ASoT** - Authoritative Source of Truth (models and data).
- **TDP** - Technical Data Package.

## System context

- **SoS** - System of Systems.
- **FoS** - Family of Systems.
- **CONOPS** - Concept of Operations.
- **OMS/MP** - Operational Mode Summary / Mission Profile.
- **HSI** - Human Systems Integration.
- **COTS** - Commercial Off-the-Shelf.
- **DMSMS** - Diminishing Manufacturing Sources and Material Shortages.
- **R&M** - Reliability and Maintainability.
- **PHS&T** - Packaging, Handling, Storage, and Transportation.
