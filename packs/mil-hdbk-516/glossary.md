# Glossary  -  MIL-HDBK-516D

Synthesized terms from section 3 definitions and acronyms, section 21 subject terms, and Appendix A reference matter. Short forms kept. Not a full dump of every handbook line.

## Core terms

- **Airframe.** Structure of the air vehicle apart from accessories and power plant (fuselage, wings, empennage, landing gear, nacelles or pods as principal parts).
- **Air system.** Air vehicle plus training and support elements (communications, control, ground/surface/control station, launch and recovery, support) and weapons employed on the air vehicle.
- **Air vehicle.** Installed equipment (hardware, software, firmware) including airframe, propulsion, vehicle and application software, CNI, navigation/guidance, computing, fire control, displays and controls, survivability, reconnaissance, automatic flight control, armament, and related onboard systems as defined in the handbook.
- **Airworthiness (AW).** Property of an air system configuration to safely attain, sustain, and complete flight IAW approved usage limits.
- **Method of compliance (MoC).** Agreed way to show a design standard is met (examination, analysis, demonstration, test, or combination).
- **Safety-critical function (SCF).** Function whose loss or malfunction can compromise safe flight or related AW outcomes as tailored in the basis.
- **Safety of flight (SOF).** Condition affirmed by AW approval that an appropriate safety level exists with accepted residual risk.
- **Configuration management (CM).** Control of the assessed configuration and related products (baselines, manuals, software loads).
- **Vehicle control function (VCF).** Safety-critical control path hardware and software for ground and flight control.
- **Electromagnetic environmental effects (E3).** Effects of the electromagnetic environment on the air system, including EMC, lightning, and p-static concerns.
- **Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC).** Ability of systems to operate without unacceptable electromagnetic interference in the intended environment.
- **Electromagnetic environment (EME).** Electromagnetic conditions in which the air system operates.
- **Computer systems and software (CS&S).** Computing hardware, software, and firmware subject to section 15 criteria when they support or affect SCFs.
- **Aerial refueling (AR).** In-flight fuel transfer between air vehicles, including boom, receptacle, drogue, and probe systems and interfaces.
- **FRIES.** Fast rope insertion and extraction systems on applicable rotary-wing air vehicles.
- **Critical safety item (CSI).** Item whose failure can have catastrophic or otherwise defined severe AW effect, with critical characteristics under special control.
- **Stores.** Expendable and non-expendable items carried internally or externally, including munitions and related carriage items as defined in section 17.
- **NDI.** Non-destructive inspection used to protect material and structural integrity assumptions.
