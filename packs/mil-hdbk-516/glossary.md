# Glossary — MIL-HDBK-516C

- **Airworthiness** — Property of an air system allowing safe flight and operation within approved limits. → ch01
- **Avionics** — Airborne electronics (and applicable UAS ground-segment avionics) supporting air vehicle functions. → ch06
- **Certification basis** — Documented set of airworthiness criteria and methods of compliance for a specific air system. → ch01
- **Certification source data** — Typical analyses, tests, and artifacts referenced to evaluate compliance with a section criteria. → ch02–ch08
- **Crew systems** — Aircrew/operator interface, life support, escape/survival, and related human-system elements. → ch08
- **Diagnostics system** — Onboard/offboard health and fault diagnostic capability tied to safe operation and maintenance. → ch06
- **E3 (Electromagnetic Environmental Effects)** — Electromagnetic environment impacts on system airworthiness. → ch06
- **Flight technology** — Flying qualities, performance, stability/control, and related flight-path disciplines. → ch04
- **Method of compliance** — Agreed way (analysis, test, inspection, simulation, etc.) to show a criterion is met. → ch01
- **Propulsion installation** — Integration of the propulsion unit with the air vehicle (inlets, mounts, controls, etc.). → ch05
- **Qualitative criteria** — Non-numeric airworthiness statements requiring engineered interpretation. → ch01
- **Tailoring** — Selecting and adjusting criteria/standards/methods to create a necessary and sufficient certification basis. → ch01
- **UAS/ROA control station** — Ground/remote operator segment that may fall under crew-systems/avionics criteria. → ch06, ch08
