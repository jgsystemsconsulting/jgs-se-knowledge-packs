# Chapter: Scope, tailoring, and notes

## Core Idea

MIL-HDBK-516D, 29 May 2026 sets airworthiness (AW) criteria for design assessment of fixed-wing and rotary-wing air systems, manned or unmanned. Each criterion pairs a standard with methods of compliance (MoCs). The handbook is guidance only: it is not cited as a contractual requirement. Component AW authorities use tailored criteria under DoDI 5030.61 when approving flight.

AW here means the property of an air system configuration to safely attain, sustain, and complete flight within approved usage limits. Criteria address design attributes of the air system. They do not address civilian harm, ground-crew safety (unless it affects AW), or as-built, as-maintained, or as-operated states outside the design assessment.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Standard plus MoC

Every criterion has a design standard and associated MoCs (examination, analysis, demonstration, test, and combinations). MoCs show how the design meets the standard before residual risk acceptance.

### Certification basis by tailoring

Programs select a necessary and sufficient subset of criteria for the air system, document applicability and MoCs, and keep the basis under configuration control. Tailoring starts early in systems engineering so assessments support milestones.

### Publication classes (section 2)

Section 2 names government and non-government publication classes used with the handbook: military specifications, standards, and handbooks; other government publications; commercial and industry standards. Names are classes and titles, not a dumped bibliography and not long quotes of those sources.

### Notes (section 21)

Section 21 states intended use across the life cycle when a design-based AW assessment is needed, lists subject terms, and notes that marginal change bars are not used because the revision is extensive.

## Key Concepts

- **DoW Component AW authority.** Issues AW approval before flight after assessment of configuration, limits, and residual risk.
- **Safety of flight (SOF).** Approval affirms an appropriate SOF level and accepted residual risk.
- **Air system vs air vehicle.** Air system includes the air vehicle plus training, support, control stations, launch/recovery, and weapons employment elements.
- **Commercial derivative aircraft and AI.** Scope covers considerations for commercial derivatives and for artificial intelligence elements in the air system; both still route through the same authority process.
- **Guidance-only status.** The cover and foreword state the handbook guides; do not cite it as a requirement.

## Mental Models

- **Design attribute, not process catalogue.** Processes appear as MoC support for design claims, not as standalone process requirements.
- **Tailor before you freeze the basis.** Unreviewed full-catalogue adoption is not the method.
- **Name standards; do not paste them.** Cited military and commercial documents stay named references.

## Key Takeaways

- Pin is MIL-HDBK-516D, 29 May 2026.
- Handbook is guidance only for design AW criteria across manned and unmanned air systems.
- Pair every used standard with MoCs and keep the basis under CM.
- Section 2 is class naming, not a quote dump.
- Sections 14 and 18 are intentionally blank and carry no criteria.
