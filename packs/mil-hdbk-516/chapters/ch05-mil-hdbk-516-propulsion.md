# Chapter: Propulsion criteria

## Core Idea

Section 7 sets minimum criteria to establish, verify, and maintain an airworthy propulsion design for manned and unmanned air systems, including ground and shipboard use. Six areas: propulsion system safety and data; performance and operability; structural integrity; components and subsystems; propeller and propeller drive; other propulsion systems.

Safety-and-data standards apply to all propulsion types (turbojet, turbofan, turboshaft, propeller-driven, reciprocating, atypical). Performance, integrity, and component standards focus on gas turbine designs and call out other types where needed.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Safety and data first

Regardless of propulsion type, capture safety-related data, limitations, and failure information that the AW assessment needs. Data quality is part of the airworthy design claim.

### Gas turbine core path

Performance and operability, structural integrity, and components/subsystems form the main gas-turbine evidence chain from thermodynamic behavior through rotating parts and accessories.

### Propeller and other systems

Propeller and propeller-drive criteria stand as their own subsection. Other propulsion systems cover designs that do not fit the gas-turbine pattern without forcing a false fit.

## Key Concepts

- **Operability.** Starts, transients, inlet compatibility, and stable operation in the intended envelope.
- **Propulsion structural integrity.** Life and integrity of engine structure under approved usage.
- **Components and subsystems.** Fuel, oil, control, ignition, and related engine subsystems as design attributes.
- **Installation interface.** Mounts, inlets, exhaust, and air-vehicle interfaces that affect AW (with structures and subsystems).
- **Shipboard and ground use.** Criteria apply when the design is intended for those environments.

## Mental Models

- **Type first, then subsection.** Pick the propulsion family before arguing MoCs.
- **Data is a design product.** Missing safety data is an AW gap, not paperwork.
- **Unit and installation.** Engine unit claims and air-vehicle installation claims both matter.

## Key Takeaways

- Section 7 spans safety/data, performance/operability, integrity, components, propellers, and other systems.
- Safety-and-data criteria apply across propulsion types.
- Gas-turbine path is the default detailed chain; propellers and other systems have dedicated coverage.
- Coordinate mounts and inlets with structures and flight technology.
