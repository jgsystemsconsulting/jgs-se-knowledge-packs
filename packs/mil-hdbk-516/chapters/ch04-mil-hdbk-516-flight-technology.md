# Chapter: Flight technology criteria

## Core Idea

Section 6 is the largest domain section. It sets flight technology AW criteria for all air systems and modifications unless a standard explicitly limits vehicle type. Four functional areas: flying qualities; vehicle control function (VCF); internal aerodynamics; external aerodynamics and performance.

Standards and MoCs cover normal and degraded states across the ground and flight envelope, including atmospheric conditions that matter for safe operation.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Four-area flight mechanics split

1. Flying qualities: handling, stability, and control in normal and degraded states.
2. VCF: safety-critical hardware and software that stabilize and control the vehicle on ground and in flight.
3. Internal aerodynamics: inlet, bay, and related internal flow effects on safe operation.
4. External aerodynamics and performance: external aero and performance needed for AW claims.

### Normal and degraded states

Criteria expect evidence in both the intended healthy state and credible degraded states. Loss of redundancy or control paths must still leave acceptable flying qualities or a safe outcome as tailored.

### Buildup MoCs

Typical path: design definition, analysis, simulation, ground test, then flight test where required. Each MoC states when it applies relative to flight test entry.

## Key Concepts

- **Flying qualities levels.** Handling quality expectations used when judging degraded hydraulic or control cases (cross-linked with subsystems).
- **VCF architecture.** Integrated control architecture, redundancy, and failure management for safety-critical control.
- **Envelope.** Ground and flight envelope including atmospheric conditions called out by the standards.
- **Performance for AW.** Performance evidence needed to show the design can attain, sustain, and complete flight within approved limits - not a full mission-performance catalogue.

## Mental Models

- **Largest section stands alone.** Do not merge flight technology into a neighbor to shrink the pack.
- **Control is a system.** VCF spans sensors, computers, actuators, and laws; software claims also touch section 15.
- **Degraded first in planning.** MoCs that only show the healthy aircraft miss the safety case.

## Key Takeaways

- Section 6 covers flying qualities, VCF, internal aero, and external aero/performance.
- Apply unless a standard explicitly carves out a vehicle type.
- Plan MoCs for normal and degraded states across the envelope.
- Coordinate with structures, propulsion, subsystems, and software chapters.
