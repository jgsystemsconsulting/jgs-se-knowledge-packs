# Chapter 1: Scope, Applicability, and Tailoring the Certification Basis

## Core Idea
MIL-HDBK-516C Section 1 defines the handbook role: qualitative airworthiness certification criteria for DoD air systems, used by the airworthiness authority to build a system-specific certification basis. Criteria are guidance and must be tailored to configuration, service, and mission.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Airworthiness certification criteria set**: Organized qualitative criteria spanning engineering domains for air-system airworthiness.
- **Certification basis**: Documented, configuration-controlled subset of applicable criteria for a specific air system.
- **Tailoring rules**: Select necessary and sufficient criteria; adjust standards/methods of compliance with qualified judgment.

## Key Concepts
- 516C provides qualitative airworthiness criteria for DoD air systems.
- A certification basis is the tailored, documented subset for one configuration.
- Tailoring selects necessary and sufficient criteria with qualified rationale.
- Methods of compliance pair each criterion with analysis, test, or inspection evidence.
- The handbook is guidance and is not raw contractual shall-text.
- UAS and commercial-derivative cases need explicit applicability judgment.

## Mental Models
- 516C is a menu for building a certification basis, not a one-size checklist to impose verbatim.
- Tailoring is disciplined selection with documentation — not silent omission.
- Terms like acceptable are defined by the implementing office for each air system context.

## Anti-patterns
- **Contractual copy-paste**: Citing the entire handbook as a shall statement.
- **Invisible tailoring**: Dropping criteria without certification-basis traceability.
- **Unqualified tailoring**: Non-specialists weakening criteria without technical rationale.

## Key Takeaways
1. Start every program by drafting a documented certification basis from 516C.
2. Apply tailoring rules with domain-qualified engineers.
3. Keep the basis under configuration control as the air system evolves.
4. Use section reference lists as compliance data hints, not exhaustive contract CDRLs.
5. Remember: guidance only — pair with service airworthiness policy.
