# Chapter: Armaments and stores integration

## Core Idea

Section 17 is the domain for putting weapons and stores on an air system safely: fit, interfaces, carriage, and use. Armament gear is the equipment set that lets those stores and weapons function as designed. Stores include expendable and non-expendable items carried internally or externally, including munitions classes named in the handbook and related stores such as fuel tanks, pods, and directed-energy weapons where applicable.

Subsections address aeromechanical integration, stores environments, armament and weapons integration, laser integration, and armament/stores technical documentation.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Carriage to employment chain

Show safe carriage, interface integrity, environmental qualification of the store on the parent air vehicle, and safe employment or jettison as designed.

### Environments on the store

Vibration, aero, thermal, electromagnetic, and related environments imposed by the parent vehicle and mission must be within the store and interface design.

### Documentation as part of integration

Technical documentation for armament and stores is an AW product: operators and maintainers need correct employment and safety data for the cleared configurations.

## Key Concepts

- **Aeromechanical integration.** Physical fit, loads, clearance, and separation characteristics.
- **Stores environments.** Environments the store sees when installed and flown.
- **Weapons integration.** Interfaces that enable safe arming, release, and employment.
- **Laser integration.** Specific criteria when lasers are part of the armament suite.
- **Technical documentation.** Manuals and data that match cleared store configurations.

## Mental Models

- **Store is part of the air system.** External and internal stores change mass properties, aero, E3, and structural loads.
- **Employment is a design claim.** Release and jettison paths need MoCs, not only carriage.
- **Name classes; do not quote specs.** Munitions and interface standards stay named references.

## Key Takeaways

- Section 17 is armaments and stores integration end to end.
- Cover aeromechanical, environments, weapons integration, lasers, and documentation.
- Reassess parent air vehicle structures, flight technology, and E3 when store sets change.
- Keep technical data aligned with cleared configurations.
