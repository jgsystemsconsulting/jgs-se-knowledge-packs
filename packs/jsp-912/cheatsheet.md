# HFI Cheatsheet

Six process stage gates and six MOD staff roles from JSP 912, described in prose as reconstructed notes. No page images, no reproduced clause text.

## The six HFI stage gates

- HFI-1.0 User Need Definition: capability-side stage in Pre-Concept and Concept, supporting the Outline Business Case. Appoint the HFIF(Cap), write the FLC HFI Plan, analyse legacy system data and feedback to build the HFI baseline dataset, and identify the Human Component of Capability: HFI input to the URD, to the CONEMP, and early Target Audience Description. Ends with the human considerations resident in the User Requirements.
- HFI-2.0 System Requirements Definition: project-side stage in late Concept and Assessment, ending at tender issue. Appoint the HFIF(PT), write the project HFI Plan, establish the MOD HFI Steering Group, define the Target Audience, run the Early Human Factors Analysis and follow-on analyses, maintain the HFI RAIDO Register, and produce the three requirement types (HFURs for the URD, HFSRs for the SRD, HFPRs for the contract), then compile contract documentation and set the HFI Tender Assessment Criteria.
- HFI-3.0 Assess Tenders: at the end of Assessment, and again for any pre-Demonstration contract. Scrutinise the tenderer's HFI Plan and compliance statements against the five fixed criteria, use independent design analysis where claims look optimistic (especially COTS/MOTS), route trades and non-compliances to the RWG and CIWG, and feed selection.
- HFI-4.0 Detailed System Design: Demonstration phase; the Solution Provider runs the design, MOD assures. Stand up the joint HFI Working Group, review and approve the Provider's HFI Plan, conduct analysis, design, and test-and-evaluation activities across the five domains, manage the jointly owned RAIDO register, handle design changes and requirement negotiations, and produce the HFI deliverables (HFI Plan, HFI Log, HFI Case Report, task analyses, HMI/HCI style guide and specifications, test specifications and reports).
- HFI-5.0 Test and Acceptance: per the ITEA Plan; the Provider tests under MOD scrutiny. Assess specifications, observe tests, review reports, assess compliance, capture new considerations in the register with evidence links, quantify and justify any non-compliances before CIWG/RWG, populate the VVRM, and accept through the PT Leader.
- HFI-6.0 In-Service Feedback: MOD-led, through life. Capture procurement lessons before the project team ramps down, gather in-service feedback from operators, maintainers, trainers and support staff, keep the RAIDO register alive, and assess every design change across the HFI domains and DLODs; feed Learning from Experience to successor projects and re-run a compressed cycle for major upgrades.

## The six MOD staff roles

- Project Team Leader: prime responsibility for HFI being successfully managed and satisfactory outcomes achieved; nominates the HFIF(PT), ensures staff are trained for HFI duties, and ensures the SRD carries sufficient HFSRs.
- HFI Focus, two posts under one name: HFIF(Cap) runs capability-side HFI in Pre-Concept and Concept, defining people-related user requirements and scanning the DLODs; HFIF(PT) runs day-to-day project HFI, tailors the activities, and balances assurance evidence against Solution Provider freedom. Both are recommended members of the CIWG.
- Requirements Management (the Requirements Manager): the FLC's representative in the project team translates User Requirements into System Requirements, keeps the Golden Thread from Defence Policy to DLOD outputs, and puts a dedicated HFI section in the SRD, guided by the HFIF roles.
- Capability Sponsor: owns capability at programme level, leads capability change planning, identifies equipment and support requirements, and decides on new equipment and support for MOD; the SRO ensures all DLODs are addressed through life on major programmes.
- Capability Integration Working Group: integrates across the DLODs; its chairperson ensures the Human Component of Capability is captured, defined, analysed and tested, and it decides requirement trades with HFI impact.
- HFI Support Function: the DE&S Engineering Group HFI Policy team owns HFI policy, standards and guidance, agrees all tailoring, and assures the process; SQEP delivery support comes from the ITS HFI Team, Dstl Human Systems Group or the Engineering Delivery Partner via the Engineering Services - Single Front Door.
