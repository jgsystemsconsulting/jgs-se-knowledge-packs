# Competencies, research ethics and conduct (Parts 1 and 2)

## Core Idea

Every member of MOD staff who undertakes HFI activities must be a Suitably Qualified and Experienced Person, defined by reference to the Human Factors Integration Functional Competence Framework. The same rule binds industry: a Solution Provider is to have all of its HFI activities carried out by SQEP, meaning professional Ergonomists or Human Factors Engineers, and/or people with considerable experience of HFI work in a Defence context. Part 1 section 4 states the directive version and Part 2 section 6 restates it as guidance, with the same named roles and thresholds; where the two parts phrase a threshold slightly differently, this chapter follows the wording of the part indicated in the Sources line above.

The competence ladder matters as much as the rule. The framework separates an Awareness level, meaning a rounded understanding of what the HFI process exists to do and why, enough to contribute appropriate input and take part in reviews, from a Practitioner level, meaning enough knowledge of good practice plus demonstrated experience to work on tasks with little more than light supervision while keeping knowledge current.

## Frameworks Introduced

- MOD staff thresholds: the HFIF(Cap) shall have, as a minimum, Awareness gained through basic training and study, with a target competence of Practitioner. The HFIF(PT) shall have, as a minimum, Practitioner; Part 2 phrases the expectation as holding at least Technical Membership of the Chartered Institute of Ergonomics and Human Factors, ideally Registered Membership. Where this is not possible, the PT Leader shall appoint or request a SQEP holding a minimum of Technical Membership of the CIEHF to support the HFIF(PT), drawn from the DE&S Internal Technical Support HFI Team or the Engineering Delivery Partner via the Engineering Services - Single Front Door. Both HFI Focus roles are management roles, so awareness and experience of Systems Engineering processes and products is recommended in addition.
- Solution Provider thresholds: Provider HFE personnel should hold, or be working towards, Registered Membership of the CIEHF. The source recognises the company itself might not be registered, as a Registered Consultancy, but MOD expects the Provider's employees to include individually registered staff. Where a Provider engages sub-contractors without their own SQEP HF professionals, the Provider's SQEPs must support those sub-contractors and manage their activities from an HFI perspective.

## Key Concepts

HFI processes conducted across the CADMID or systems engineering lifecycles might involve research trials, experiments, tests, surveys or other forms of assessment with human participants. Where they do, JSP 536 applies: it is the MOD policy covering research that involves human participants, and the activities must comply with it. Part 2 adds that JSP 536's Annex 1A supplies a checklist teams can use to decide whether a protocol has to go forward for Scientific and Ethics Review. JSP 536 is named here, not reproduced; use its own publication for its requirements.

Beyond the JSP, HFI research activities may need to conform to the British Psychological Society's Code of Human Research Ethics and the CIEHF's Code of Professional Conduct. In such cases, the involvement of SQEP is essential. The chapter's division of labour is therefore two-layered: competence rules decide who may plan and run HFI work, and ethics rules decide how any work involving human participants is reviewed and conducted.

## Mental Models

- Awareness inputs, Practitioner leads: Awareness-level people participate and review; Practitioner-level people work unsupervised; the two HFI Focus roles are staffed accordingly, with the project-side role held to the higher floor.
- Escalate to a SQEP, never to hope: where the appointed individual cannot meet the threshold, the source prescribes a named support route rather than acceptance of the gap.

## Key Takeaways

- SQEP is mandatory on both sides of the contract, with the HFI Functional Competence Framework as the reference definition.
- HFIF(Cap) floor is Awareness with Practitioner as target; HFIF(PT) floor is Practitioner with CIEHF membership expectations.
- Human-participant research in HFI triggers JSP 536 compliance, and may additionally engage the BPS and CIEHF codes, always with SQEP involvement.
