# Glossary — DOT&E / T&E Enterprise Guidebook (8.02)

- **CDT (Chief Developmental Tester)** — Program lead integrating DT planning and execution with the Lead DT organization. → ch01, ch02
- **CT (Contractor Testing)** — Testing performed by the contractor; government reuses rather than duplicates where possible. → ch01, ch02
- **DOT&E** — Director, Operational Test and Evaluation; OSD principal for OT&E/LFT&E independence and oversight-list approvals. → ch01, ch03, ch04
- **DT&E** — Developmental Test & Evaluation; technical knowledge and risk reduction during development. → ch02
- **EOA / OA** — Early Operational Assessment / Operational Assessment on the operational event ladder. → ch03
- **FOT&E** — Follow-on OT&E after IOT&E to confirm fixes and assess changes. → ch03
- **FUSL** — Full-Up System-Level live-fire testing of a combat-equipped production-representative system. → ch04
- **IOT&E** — Initial OT&E; statutory operational evaluation under realistic conditions. → ch03
- **LFT&E** — Live Fire Test & Evaluation of survivability and lethality. → ch04
- **MOSA** — Modular Open Systems Approach; affects interface/increment testability and technical risk. → ch06
- **OTA** — Operational Test Agency (Service/agency independent OT organization). → ch01, ch03
- **OTRR** — Operational Test Readiness Review gate before operational events. → ch03, ch07
- **STE** — System test environments/infrastructure and related resources enabling T&E execution. → ch08
- **T&E Oversight List** — Joint DOT&E + USD(R&E) list changing Strategy/plan approval paths. → ch01, ch08
- **T&E Strategy / TEMP** — Planning spine for multi-community T&E; TEMP is a common documentation vehicle. → ch08
- **T&E WIPT** — Working IPT coordinating T&E stakeholders at program level. → ch01
- **USD(R&E)** — Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering; DT&E/infrastructure leadership. → ch01, ch02
