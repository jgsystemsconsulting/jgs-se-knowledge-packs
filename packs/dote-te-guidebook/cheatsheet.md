# Cheatsheet — DOT&E / T&E Enterprise Guidebook (8.02)

## Decision rules
1. **Guidance vs policy?** Defer to DoDI 5000.89 / 5000-series and Title 10 on conflict.
2. **On oversight list?** Expect DOT&E Strategy/plan approvals and tighter OT controls.
3. **Need technical risk or mission utility?** DT&E vs OT&E (plus LFT&E for survivability/lethality).
4. **Entering OT?** Pass OTRR on R/M/S and safety manageability—not slides alone.
5. **Waiving live-fire?** Only FUSL/end-to-end form with alternative plan—not LFT&E itself.
6. **Software MVP/MVCR?** Risk-informed independent test; cyber survivability in scope.
7. **Which pack?** Pathway deep-dive -> dod-te-guidebook; DOT&E/enterprise/cyber/MOSA/suitability/strategy -> this pack.
8. **Shared data enough?** Supports evaluations; does not erase dedicated OT/LFT needs.
