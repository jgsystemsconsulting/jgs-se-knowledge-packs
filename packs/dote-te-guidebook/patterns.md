# Patterns — DOT&E / T&E Enterprise Guidebook (8.02)

## 1. Oversight-First Planning
- **When:** Starting Strategy/TEMP or OT plan drafting.
- **How:** Determine T&E Oversight List status; set DOT&E vs Component approval paths before content freeze.
- **Trade-offs:** Early bureaucracy vs late rejection of entire plan packages.

## 2. Integrated Multi-Community Events
- **When:** Resource-constrained schedules across CT/DT/OT/LFT.
- **How:** Design events that feed multiple evaluations; still preserve dedicated OT/LFT scope required for independence.
- **Trade-offs:** Coordination cost vs duplicated trials.

## 3. Contract for Data, Not Just Articles
- **When:** Pre-MS B / pathway entry contracting.
- **How:** Encode government test involvement, data rights, repository access, and instrumentation in contracts.
- **Trade-offs:** Negotiation time vs blind DT/OT later.

## 4. Cyber Lifecycle Pairing
- **When:** Any networked or software-enabled system.
- **How:** Plan cooperative cyber DT and adversarial cyber OT; include pipelines when connected to production.
- **Trade-offs:** Continuous cyber investment vs single late surprise.

## 5. Reliability Growth Before OTRR
- **When:** Approaching first major operational event.
- **How:** Show failure trends, corrective actions, R/M/S and safety manageability evidence at OTRR.
- **Trade-offs:** Schedule holds vs invalid OT on immature articles.

## 6. Digital Evidence with VV&A
- **When:** Heavy M&S/automation in the Strategy.
- **How:** Budget VV&A, shared environments, and analytics; map which decisions accept M&S vs require live test.
- **Trade-offs:** Model upkeep vs untrusted digital claims.

## 7. FUSL Waiver Discipline
- **When:** Full-up live-fire is impractical.
- **How:** Pursue only FUSL/end-to-end waiver with certified alternative LFT&E plan grounded in actual testing—never "waive LFT&E."
- **Trade-offs:** Documentation burden vs statutory noncompliance.

## 8. Dual-Pack Routing (dote vs dod-te)
- **When:** Agent/user chooses between T&E packs.
- **How:** Use dod-te-guidebook for pathway chapter depth; use dote-te-guidebook for DOT&E-facing enterprise, cyber/MOSA/suitability/strategy emphasis; both cite same Aug 2022 guidebook family.
- **Trade-offs:** Two packs to maintain vs clearer routing and cluster coverage.
