# Chapter 1: T&E Enterprise Overview and Roles

## Core Idea
The DoD Test & Evaluation Enterprise Guidebook (August 2022, edition 8.02) is guidance that clarifies how to meet DoDI 5000.89 T&E policy across Adaptive Acquisition Framework pathways. It is jointly framed by USD(R&E) and DOT&E and organizes content into Overview, Pathway Guidance, and evolving Focus Areas/Companion Guides. This pack uses the DOT&E-facing enterprise view while remaining the same public guidebook family already partially covered by dod-te-guidebook.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Three-part guidebook structure**: Overview (types, docs, orgs, oversight, management); Pathway Guidance (UCA, MTA, MCA, Software, DBS; Services under review); Focus Areas/Companion Guides (e.g., cybersecurity T&E).
- **Guidance vs policy**: On conflict, defer to DoDI 5000-series and statute—not this guidebook.
- **Integrated T&E enterprise**: Contractor testing (CT), DT&E, OT&E, and LFT&E should be integrated, streamlined, automated, and reused with shared data repositories.
- **OSD split**: USD(R&E) leads DT&E/infrastructure; DOT&E leads OT&E/LFT&E policy and independent reporting; Components field Executives and OTAs.

## Key Concepts
- **Purpose of T&E**: Produce data demonstrating technical/functional/warfighting capability and expose deficiencies before fielding decisions; effectiveness hinges on planned/resourced strategy plus access to contractor data/tools.
- **Early engagement**: Involve T&E organizations from inception so contracts capture data rights/processes and tempo supports decisions via digital engineering, sequential testing, and automation.
- **DOT&E role**: Independent OT&E/LFT&E oversight, strategy/plan approval for oversight-list programs, adequacy opinions, and reports to SecDef/Congress.
- **USD(R&E) role**: DT&E policy/sufficiency, test infrastructure (with TRMC), engineering integration.
- **Program roles**: PM owns strategy resourcing; T&E WIPT coordinates; CDT leads developmental test integration; Lead DT organization and Lead OTA execute.
- **T&E Oversight List**: Joint DOT&E + USD(R&E) product that flips approval authorities and OT plan regimes.
- **Relation to dod-te-guidebook**: Same August 2022 Enterprise Guidebook source family; that pack emphasizes pathway chapters and OUSD(R&E) framing. This pack emphasizes DOT&E-facing enterprise roles, cyber/MOSA/automation/suitability depth, and cross-routing.

## Mental Models
- **Independence by design**: Developers do not solely judge operational effectiveness/suitability/survivability/lethality.
- **Collect once, answer many**: Shared events and repositories feed multiple evaluations without erasing dedicated OT/LFT events.
- **Read oversight status first**: It changes who approves the Strategy and OT plans.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating the guidebook as binding law over DoDI 5000.89.
- Duplicating the entire dod-te-guidebook pathway catalog without adding enterprise/DOT&E value.
- Excluding OT/LFT communities until late DT.

## Key Takeaways
1. Guidebook = DoDI 5000.89 implementation guidance with Overview + Pathways + Focus Areas.
2. CT/DT/OT/LFT should integrate with shared data while preserving independent OT/LFT judgments.
3. DOT&E and USD(R&E) split OT/LFT vs DT/infrastructure leadership.
4. Use alongside dod-te-guidebook rather than as a conflicting second policy.
