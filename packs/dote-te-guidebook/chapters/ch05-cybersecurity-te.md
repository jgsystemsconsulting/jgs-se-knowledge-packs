# Chapter 5: Cybersecurity T&E

## Core Idea
Cybersecurity T&E is a first-class enterprise focus: cooperative and adversarial cybersecurity testing runs throughout the lifecycle, and Focus Area/Companion Guide material expands how-to depth beyond the Overview. Survivability evaluations increasingly treat cyber as inseparable from mission outcomes.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Lifecycle cyber T&E**: Cooperative testing in development plus adversarial testing as systems mature toward operational use.
- **Focus Area + Companion Guide model**: Guidebook points to Cyber T&E Focus Area and companion how-to material rather than freezing every tactic in the main volume.
- **Mission-risk framing**: Evaluate security of the acquisition program in terms of mission failure risk, not only compliance checklists.

## Key Concepts
- **DT cyber**: Find vulnerabilities early; include cyber in DT sufficiency thinking (with reliability and interoperability).
- **OT cyber**: Operational survivability includes cyber; OTA assessments for software releases explicitly include cyber survivability.
- **Pipeline/supply chain**: For software pathways, cyber testing may include the software pipeline itself when pre-production environments connect to production—part of supply-chain assessment.
- **Embedded software**: Align MVCR cyber/OT with platform IOT&E or applicable OT&E when software rides on a host system.
- **Shared data hygiene**: Repositories and digital engineering environments used for T&E must themselves be protected and access-controlled.

## Mental Models
- **If cyber is only a late OT surprise, DT failed its early-warning job.**
- **Adversarial testing answers different questions than cooperative scanning.**

## Anti-patterns
- Deferring all cyber T&E to a single pre-IOT&E event.
- Ignoring pipeline/connectivity attack surface in software acquisition.
- Treating cyber as IA paperwork disconnected from mission threads.

## Key Takeaways
1. Cyber T&E spans cooperative DT and adversarial OT across the lifecycle.
2. Focus Area/companion guides hold detailed methods.
3. Software pathways must scope cyber for MVP/MVCR and pipelines.
4. Cyber is part of operational survivability judgments.
