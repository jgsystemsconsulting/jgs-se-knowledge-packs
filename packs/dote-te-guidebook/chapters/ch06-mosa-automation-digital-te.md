# Chapter 6: MOSA, Automation, and Digital T&E Enablers

## Core Idea
The enterprise guidebook pushes digital engineering tools, rigorous V&V, and automation to expedite T&E planning, data analysis, reporting, and management of design shortfalls. Modular Open Systems Approach (MOSA) appears in technical risk and sufficiency contexts because openness and modularity change how interfaces and increments are tested.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Digital engineering as T&E tempo tool**: Models, simulations, and automated analysis support earlier and more frequent evidence—not a substitute for required dedicated OT/LFT events.
- **Automation and sequential testing**: Maintain decision tempo with automated data collection/analysis and sequential test designs where appropriate.
- **MOSA-related risk lens**: MOSA, software, security, manufacturing, sustainment, and testing adequacy interact in technical risk assessments (e.g., ITRA-related considerations under engineering policy).

## Key Concepts
- **Shared environments**: Collaborate on digital system models, simulations, and test environments for common use across CT/DT/OT/LFT.
- **VV&A**: Verification, validation, and accreditation expectations for M&S used in T&E decisions; follow Focus Area/companion guidance.
- **Data stores / knowledge management**: Build the body of evidence for agile T&E; PM enables repositories usable by independent assessors.
- **Interface and increment testability**: MOSA-friendly architectures should make interface verification and incremental OT scoping easier—if contracts and ICDs keep pace.
- **Reporting shortfalls**: Automation should accelerate identification and tracking of design shortfalls and vulnerabilities into engineering backlogs.

## Mental Models
- **Digital twins that are not VV&A'd are storytelling, not evidence.**
- **Open architecture without test hooks is open only on paper.**

## Anti-patterns
- Claiming M&S replaces IOT&E or FUSL without authority.
- Automating dashboards while contracts still block government data access.
- Ignoring MOSA impacts on regression and integration test scope.

## Key Takeaways
1. Digital engineering and automation are enterprise enablers for T&E tempo and reuse.
2. VV&A and shared repositories keep digital evidence trustworthy and independent.
3. MOSA changes interface/increment test design and risk posture.
4. Enablers support—not erase—statutory OT/LFT duties.
