# Chapter 2: Developmental Test & Evaluation (DT&E)

## Core Idea
DT&E is the disciplined generation of substantiated knowledge about a system's capabilities and limitations during development. It verifies critical technical parameters and KPPs, manages development risk, finds cyber and other vulnerabilities early, and informs readiness for operational test.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **DT&E knowledge mission**: Technical progress and risk characterization against CONOPS/OMS/MP, capabilities documents, and technical/contractual requirements.
- **Contractor testing leverage**: Government DT reuses CT data rather than blindly re-running equivalent events.
- **DT sufficiency assessments**: For applicable MDAPs, Milestone B (and related) DT&E sufficiency looks at TEMP DT plans, schedule vs analogs, resources (facilities, people, assets, analytics, M&S), concurrency risk mitigation, and production-entry DT criteria—with reliability, interoperability, and cybersecurity focus.

## Key Concepts
- **Government test team early presence**: Ensure contracts generate required data and shared repositories exist for independent assessment.
- **Digital engineering / M&S**: Common models, simulations, and environments support spectrum of tests; VV&A expectations point to Focus Area/companion material.
- **Integration with OT**: Coordinate so DT events can contribute usable data to operational evaluations without replacing dedicated OT.
- **Chief Developmental Tester (CDT)**: Program focal point for DT planning/integration with the Lead DT organization.
- **Cyber in DT**: Cooperative cybersecurity testing begins in development—not only at OT.

## Mental Models
- **DT proves buildability and technical maturity; OT proves mission utility under threat.**
- **Sufficiency is about the test program design, not a single score.**

## Anti-patterns
- Waiting for OT to discover basic technical shortfalls DT should have found.
- Planning DT without data-rights and repository clauses.
- Ignoring reliability/interoperability/cyber in sufficiency thinking.

## Key Takeaways
1. DT&E produces technical knowledge and risk reduction toward OT readiness.
2. Reuse CT and shared data; keep government independent assessment capability.
3. Sufficiency assessments scrutinize DT plans, schedule, resources, and concurrency.
4. CDT + Lead DT organization own developmental execution.
