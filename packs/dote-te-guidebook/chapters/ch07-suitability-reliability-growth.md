# Chapter 7: Suitability and Reliability Growth

## Core Idea
Operational suitability and reliability growth are enterprise outcomes, not afterthoughts. OTRR explicitly checks reliability/maintainability/supportability maturity, DT sufficiency emphasizes reliability planning, and OT judges suitability under realistic ops and support conditions.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Suitability as OT outcome**: Can units operate and sustain the system with expected manpower, training, support equipment, and logistics under mission conditions?
- **Reliability growth in DT**: Plan and resource reliability testing/analysis early; sufficiency reviews examine whether reliability risk is actually being retired.
- **OTRR reliability gate**: Entering operational events without manageable reliability/maintainability/supportability and safety posture wastes OT and risks inadequate evaluation.

## Key Concepts
- **R&M engineering linkage**: Reliability and maintainability requirements need testable measures and failure-reporting loops into design.
- **Supportability / logistics T&E**: Training, tech manuals, spares, tools, and facilities are suitability evidence sources—coordinate with logistics stakeholders (cluster 23).
- **Hazard/ESOH/software safety**: OTRR includes manageability of hazards and software safety, not only mean-time metrics.
- **Growth curves and fixes**: Reliability growth requires closed-loop corrective action, not endless testing of an unfixed design.
- **Pathway speed pressure**: Urgent/MTA/software tempos still need suitability risk visibility; tailor evidence, do not invent zero-suitability programs.

## Mental Models
- **If it only works with contractor field service reps forever, suitability is failing.**
- **OTRR is a suitability gate disguised as a schedule milestone.**

## Anti-patterns
- Deferring reliability testing until after IOT&E planning is complete.
- Measuring only laboratory reliability without ops/support context.
- Passing OTRR on briefing charts without failure-data trends.

## Key Takeaways
1. Suitability is a core OT evaluation outcome tied to real support concepts.
2. Reliability growth must be planned, resourced, and sufficiency-checked in DT.
3. OTRR enforces R/M/S and safety manageability before operational events.
4. Logistics and training evidence belong in the T&E body of knowledge.
