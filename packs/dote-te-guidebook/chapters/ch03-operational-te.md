# Chapter 3: Operational Test & Evaluation (OT&E)

## Core Idea
OT&E evaluates how units equipped with the system perform under realistic operational conditions and threats to judge operational effectiveness, suitability, and survivability (statutory anchor 10 U.S.C. 4171; policy DoDI 5000.89). DOT&E is the OSD principal for OT&E independence and oversight-list approvals.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Operational event ladder**: Operational Demonstration -> Early Operational Assessment (EOA) -> Operational Assessment (OA) -> Initial OT&E (IOT&E) -> Follow-on OT&E (FOT&E).
- **OTRR gate**: Operational Test Readiness Review checks reliability, maintainability, supportability, and hazard/ESOH/software-safety manageability before operational events.
- **Oversight-driven approvals**: On the T&E Oversight List, DOT&E approves relevant strategies/plans and may require advance submission and concurrence before deviations.

## Key Concepts
- **IOT&E**: Statutory production-representative, end-to-end testing under realistic combat conditions; informs full-rate production decisions; DOT&E adequacy/opinion reporting applies for covered programs.
- **EOA/OA**: Early and progressive operational risk reads using analysis, early DT, M&S, and increasingly realistic conditions; EOA plans for oversight programs approved by DOT&E.
- **FOT&E**: Confirms fixes, evaluates untested aspects, assesses modifications after IOT&E.
- **Lead OTA**: Service/agency operational test agency executes independent OT; coordinates with PM/WIPT without losing independence.
- **Shared data does not mean lost independence**: OT should integrate usable data from all sources while retaining appropriately scoped dedicated tests.
- **Software pathway nuance**: MVP/MVCR scoping uses risk-informed independent government test; OTA assesses progress toward effectiveness/suitability/survivability including cyber.

## Mental Models
- **OT answers mission questions users and commanders care about, not only spec compliance.**
- **Readiness review before OT prevents testing immature systems as if they were fieldable.**

## Anti-patterns
- Calling contractor demos "IOT&E."
- Skipping OTRR because schedule pressure.
- Assuming pathway speed removes OT independence requirements.

## Key Takeaways
1. OT&E judges effectiveness, suitability, survivability under realistic conditions.
2. Event ladder + OTRR structure maturity toward IOT&E/FOT&E.
3. DOT&E oversight changes approval and reporting pathways.
4. Lead OTA executes; shared data supports but does not replace dedicated OT.
