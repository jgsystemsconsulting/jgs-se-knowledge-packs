# Chapter 6: Reviews, Responsibilities, and Contractor Requirements

## Core Idea
The Order distributes accountability across line managers, project leads, oversight bodies, and contractors. External and independent project reviews, clear role definitions (appendices/attachments), and the Contractor Requirements Document (CRD) ensure that federal direction becomes executable contract duty. Closeout and lessons learned complete the control loop.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Roles and responsibilities model**: Appendix/attachment mapping of duties for key federal actors and support offices.
- **Project reviews**: Peer, independent, and external reviews timed to risk and CD milestones.
- **Contractor Requirements Document (CRD)**: Attachment that flows Order requirements into contracts.
- **Oversight and support offices**: PM, PMSO, and related organizations enabling and checking line management.
- **Completion/closeout discipline**: Transition to operations, physical and financial closeout, lessons learned.
- **Invoked standards and references**: Technical standards and external references the Order points to without fully restating.

## Key Concepts
- **Line management accountability**: Ultimate delivery ownership stays with line, not reviewers.
- **Independent voice**: Reviews surface issues line teams may normalize.
- **CRD incorporation**: Requirements bite contractors only when properly in the contract.
- **Documentation sufficiency**: Reports may be combined but must meet intent and audit needs.
- **Lessons learned feed-forward**: Closeout knowledge should change future planning and risk.
- **Interface to quality and operating-experience programs**: Broader DOE directives ecosystem.

## Mental Models
- Reviews are sensors; line management is the actuator.
- If it is not in the CRD/contract, contractor obligation is ambiguous.
- Closeout is a project phase with acceptance criteria, not an administrative afterthought.
- Responsibilities appendices answer “who?” when the main text answers “what?”

## Anti-patterns
- **Review tourism**: Many reviews, no decision changes.
- **CRD copy-paste without scope fit**: Unenforceable or wrong-sized requirements.
- **Federal desk rejecting ownership because “the IPT decided”**: Line still owns.
- **Closeout without financial/physical completion evidence**: Lingering liabilities.

## Key Takeaways
1. Know the responsibility map for FPD, PSO, PMSO, PM, and approvers.
2. Plan independent/external reviews as part of CD readiness, not surprises.
3. Flow requirements to contractors through a proper CRD/contract path.
4. Keep line management accountable for outcomes.
5. Execute completion/closeout with evidence and lessons learned.
6. Use invoked standards/references deliberately; do not assume the Order reprints them.
