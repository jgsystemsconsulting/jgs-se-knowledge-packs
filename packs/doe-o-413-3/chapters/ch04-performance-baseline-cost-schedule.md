# Chapter 4: Performance Baseline, Cost, Schedule, and Earned Value

## Core Idea
After CD-2, the performance baseline is the commitment contract between the project and the Department. Cost estimating, schedule integrity, contingency/management reserve logic, change control, and earned value management (where required) are the machinery that keeps delivery honest against that baseline and visible in PARS.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Performance Baseline (PB)**: Approved scope, cost, and schedule snapshot used for performance measurement.
- **Baseline management / change control**: Controlled evolution of the PB when facts change.
- **Cost estimating expectations**: Credible estimates supporting ranges (CD-1) and PB (CD-2).
- **Planning and scheduling discipline**: Integrated schedules that support critical path and forecasting.
- **Earned Value Management System (EVMS)**: Required earned-value reporting after CD-2 for covered projects (Order thresholds/conditions).
- **PARS transparency**: Department reporting system for progress and documentation at/after CD-0 for covered projects.

## Key Concepts
- **TPC (Total Project Cost)**: Roll-up cost figure used in thresholds and authority.
- **Contingency vs management reserve**: Risk-related funds with different ownership/use rules (per Order/guides).
- **Directed change**: External direction that can legitimately move the PB goalposts.
- **Estimate confidence and documentation**: Traceable basis of estimate, not a single spreadsheet cell.
- **Schedule health**: Logic-driven schedules, credible float, status discipline.
- **Performance measurement baseline inside EVMS**: Technical link between PB and EV data.

## Mental Models
- The PB is a promise with a change-control door — not a wish and not an immovable monolith.
- EVMS without schedule/cost integration is theater.
- Contingency is risk funding, not a slush fund for poor definition.
- PARS makes performance a Department-visible fact.

## Anti-patterns
- **Silent baseline drift**: Scope grows, PB unchanged, EV green-washed.
- **CD-2 estimate = sales number**: Unachievable PB from day one.
- **EV reporting without validated system**: Numbers without integrity.
- **Ignoring directed vs self-inflicted change**: Mis-classifying variances.

## Key Takeaways
1. CD-2 freezes the PB that defines success.
2. Manage baseline changes through formal control, not informal hope.
3. Build cost and schedule products that can survive scrutiny.
4. Apply EVMS when the Order requires it post-CD-2.
5. Use PARS as the Department window into progress.
6. Separate contingency/MR usage from ordinary overruns.
