# Chapter 4: M&S Program Manager's Role in VV&A of New Simulations

## Core Idea
The M&S PM integrates VV&A into the development program: scope, schedule, budget, contractors, risk, and decision gates so accreditation is a planned outcome rather than a surprise.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **VV&A program integration**: VV&A plan aligned to acquisition or development milestones.
- **Resource triad**: people (including independent V&V), tools/environments, and calendar time for validation events.
- **Risk-managed tailoring**: depth of VV&A scaled to consequence, novelty, and reuse (links ch10).

## Key Concepts
- **VV&A planning authority**: ensure a living VV&A plan exists with roles, products, and exit criteria.
- **Contracting for evidence**: SOW/CDRLs that require developer V&V support, data rights, and access for independent agents.
- **Coordination**: User needs, developer increments, V&V events, and accreditation package timing.
- **Issue escalation**: technical or independence conflicts raised before accreditation deadlines.
- **Reuse and legacy**: when incorporating prior M&S, plan delta-VV&A for the new intended use.

## Mental Models
- **Critical path includes credibility**: validation windows are schedule items, not slack fillers.
- **Buy access, not just code**: without data rights and instrumentation, V&V fails regardless of budget size.
- **Single throat for integration**: PM prevents role fragmentation from becoming gaps in evidence.

## Anti-patterns
- Funding development fully and VV&A residually.
- Appointing the developer organization alone as V&V Agent without independence rationale.
- Freezing the accreditation date while leaving intended use unstable.
- Ignoring configuration drift between “validated build” and “fielded build.”

## Key Takeaways
1. PMs make VV&A executable through plans, money, contracts, and gates.
2. Independence and access are procurement problems as much as technical ones.
3. Tailor rigor to risk; do not tailor away intended-use clarity.
4. Accreditation timing is a program milestone owned by the PM with the Accreditation Agent.
