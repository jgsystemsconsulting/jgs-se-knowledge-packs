# Chapter 7: Implementation, Numbering, Reporting, and Sustainment

## Core Idea
Section 4 and Appendices L–M close the loop: implement the Contract WBS after award, keep numbering and reporting coherent, and—new emphasis in 881F—use Appendix L to report sustainment costs with the CAPE Sustainment Cost Estimating Structure while Appendix M details Government System Test and Evaluation breakouts.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Post-award implementation** — manage, extend, and control the Contract WBS as the technical and contractual baseline evolves.
- **Numbering and indenture** — consistent hierarchical identifiers so CSDR, IMS, and technical documents point to the same nodes.
- **Reporting levels** — negotiated between Government and contractor; definition depth may exceed reporting depth.
- **Appendix L Sustainment** — formal (not information-only) appendix aligning sustainment cost reporting to CAPE Sustainment CES; K commons are not a substitute for L.
- **Appendix M Government ST&E** — Government system test and evaluation element definitions for appropriate programs.
- **881F delta awareness** — L upgraded from info-only; K applicability clarified as A–J (not L/M).

## Key Concepts
- **WBS in EVMS/CSDR** — control accounts and reporting structures map to WBS elements without inventing conflicting parents.
- **Change control** — WBS revisions track engineering changes and contract mods; dictionary updates travel with structural changes.
- **Sustainment vs acquisition WBS** — O&S reporting under L uses CES logic; do not force pure acquisition A–J trees to answer every O&S question.
- **Government test ownership** — M clarifies Government-performed ST&E products/services for visibility.
- **Crosswalk discipline** — when legacy 881E structures exist, map carefully—especially L and K boundary changes.
- **Keyword/subject terms** — use standard subject terms from the document for search and ASSIST hygiene.

## Mental Models
- **One identifier, many views** — cost, schedule, and technical views are projections of one WBS.
- **Report what you manage; define what you risk** — high-risk nodes defined even if not on every report.
- **881F L is real work** — treat sustainment CES alignment as a first-class implementation task.

## Anti-patterns
- Renumbering casually between CDRLs so historic data breaks.
- Ignoring Appendix L because 'we already have K logistics elements'.
- Reporting WBS that does not match the managed Contract WBS.
- No dictionary update when elements split/merge after PDR/CDR.

## Key Takeaways
1. Implementation keeps Contract WBS alive under configuration control.
2. Numbering/reporting must stay synchronized with management structure.
3. Appendix L is 881F's sustainment CES reporting backbone.
4. Appendix M covers Government ST&E; K remains A–J commons.
