# Glossary: Manufacturing Readiness Level Deskbook (Version 2025)

Short definitions for Version 2025 deskbook vocabulary. Appendix E acronyms are absorbed here. Role pair terms come from Section 1.

## Role pair (Section 1)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Responsible organization | Project or program office, industry or government, that owns cost, schedule, and performance for the acquisition activity. |
| Assessed organization | Organization, industry or government, that is the subject of an MRL Assessment. |

## Core terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Manufacturing Readiness Level (MRL) | Ordinal label for a set of manufacturing maturity criteria and metrics used to discuss and assess manufacturing risk. |
| MRL Assessment | Evaluation of manufacturing risk and maturity using MRL criteria and metrics (AS6500 framing). Sometimes called a Manufacturing Readiness Assessment. |
| Manufacturing Maturation Plan (MMP) | Follow-on plan that addresses each assessment shortfall versus target maturity. |
| Thread | One of nine primary manufacturing-risk categories in Section 2.4. |
| Sub-thread | Finer risk slice inside a thread, tracked across levels. |
| Demonstration environment | Setting where maturity is shown: laboratory, production-relevant, production-representative, pilot line, or production line. |
| Adaptive Acquisition Framework (AAF) | DoD pathway set that lets decision authorities match process to the capability being acquired. |
| Major Capability Acquisition (MCA) | Traditional major-system pathway inside the AAF. |
| Middle Tier Acquisition (MTA) | Rapid Prototyping and Rapid Fielding pathway set. |
| Urgent Capability Acquisition (UCA) | Compressed pathway for urgent operational needs. |
| Technology Readiness Level (TRL) | Scale for technology maturity; related to but distinct from MRL. |
| Operational Technology (OT) | Programmable shop-floor systems and devices that sense or change the physical world; screened inside Version 2025 assessments. |
| AS6500A | Industry manufacturing management standard related to MRL thread activities in contract language. |
| Users Guide | External Excel workbook that supplements the deskbook for interactive assessment support. |
| Criteria matrix (Appendix A) | Detailed thread and sub-thread criteria across levels; not reproduced cell by cell in this pack. |
