# Cheatsheet: Manufacturing Readiness Level Deskbook (Version 2025)

Quick reference for Version 2025 structure. Details live in chapters.

## Role pair

- **Responsible organization:** owns cost, schedule, performance.
- **Assessed organization:** subject of the MRL Assessment.

## Ten-level bands

| Band | Focus |
|---|---|
| MRL 1-4 | Pre-systems through solution selection |
| MRL 5-6 | Early prototypes toward preliminary design |
| MRL 7 | Production-representative environment toward detailed design |
| MRL 8 | Pilot-line proof |
| MRL 9 | Full-rate production transition |
| MRL 10 | Lean and continuous improvement in production |

Numbers are targets on a non-linear ordinal scale, not grades.

## Environments

Laboratory → production-relevant → production-representative → pilot line → production line.

## Pathway targets (orientation)

| Pathway / phase cue | Orientation target language |
|---|---|
| MSA / early MCA | MRL Assessments (MRL 4) |
| End of TMRR pressure | should also be at least MRL 6 |
| EMD toward LRIP | appropriate level of maturity for LRIP; Best practices call for a target of MRL 8 for LRIP |
| FRP | appropriate level of maturity for FRP; objective of meeting MRL 9 |
| MTA Rapid Prototyping | minimum target of MRL 4 |
| MTA Rapid Fielding | will target MRL 8; of meeting MRL 8 criteria |
| UCA production-representative | manufacturing maturity should be MRL 7 |
| Residual industrial gap | fall short of MRL 6 |

Exact placement depends on pathway and product. See Chapter 3.

## Assessment spine (Section 4)

## MMP essentials (Section 5)

Problem, options, schedule, funding, actions, prototypes, thresholds, open criteria. One MMP per missed target element.

## Contract hooks (Section 6)

Acquisition plan targets → RFP Section L instructions → Section M factors → SOW tasking → DIDs for assessment and MMP → quality and AS6500A alignment. Criteria describe; contracts task.
