# Chapter: Introduction to the Version 2025 MRL Deskbook

## Core Idea

The Version 2025 Manufacturing Readiness Level Deskbook is a body of practice for judging manufacturing maturity and manufacturing risk during acquisition. It is written for people who run MRL Assessments, for program managers, systems engineers, manufacturing managers, and leaders of technology development or pre-systems demonstration work.

This document is not a DoD requirement and is offered as a best practice. It does not replace statute, DoD policy, the FAR, or DFARS. Criteria describe what maturity looks like. They do not, by themselves, task a contractor.

Version 2025 retires a Justification subsection that earlier editions carried. Do not restore it. Page 2 of the pinned edition also records that former numbered sections on the Users Guide and criteria adaptation moved into appendices, and that operational-technology cybersecurity plus the acronym list moved into appendices as well.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Responsible organization and assessed organization

The deskbook uses two role labels that travel through later chapters:

- **Responsible organization.** The project or program office, government or industry, that owns cost, schedule, and performance for the acquisition activity.
- **Assessed organization.** The organization, government or industry, that is the subject of the MRL Assessment.

Local lexicons may differ. Keep the pair as deskbook vocabulary when reading assessment, MMP, and contract chapters.

### Document map

Version 2025 organizes body and appendices as:

1. Scale, environments, threads, and the MRL to TRL relationship (Section 2).
2. Targets inside the Adaptive Acquisition Framework (Section 3).
3. How to run an assessment (Section 4).
4. Manufacturing Maturation Plans and risk practice (Section 5).
5. Contract language and AS6500A relationship (Section 6).
6. Appendix A holds the criteria matrix (not reproduced cell by cell in this pack).
7. Appendix B is the Users Guide for performing assessments (Excel workbook stays external).
8. Appendix C covers adapting criteria (S and T, sustainment and depot, limited systems including ships, industry).
9. Appendix D covers operational-technology cybersecurity screening inside an assessment.
10. Appendix E is the acronym list (absorbed into the pack glossary).

### Assessment purpose

An MRL Assessment, as AS6500 frames it, evaluates manufacturing risk and maturity against MRL criteria and metrics. Practitioners sometimes call the same activity a Manufacturing Readiness Assessment. In either label, the job is to establish current maturity, name shortfalls with their risks and costs, and support maturation management.

## Key Concepts

- **Best-practice uses.** Identify risks, issues, and opportunities; reduce manufacturing risk before production transition; inject manufacturing judgment earlier into design and decision work; keep application flexible across industry, contractors, and government products.
- **Audience.** Assessors, PMs, systems engineers, manufacturing managers, and managers of technology development or pre-systems demonstration projects.
- **BoK intent.** The deskbook pulls manufacturing language and standards into one reference set. Organizational synonyms are expected; the role pair above is the deskbook default.
- **Named policy backdrop.** DoDI 5000.88 speaks to production, quality, and manufacturing risk across the life cycle. Name the instruction. Do not rebuild or quote its text here.
- **What Version 2025 changed in structure.** Justification is gone. Users Guide, criteria adaptation, OT cybersecurity, and acronyms sit in appendices B through E. Numbered body stops at Section 6.

## Mental Models

**Advisory, not mandate.** Treat every procedure in this pack as practice guidance. Contract tasking comes from the acquisition documents Chapter 6 names, not from a criteria cell alone.

**Role split drives who writes what.** The responsible organization sets scope, reviews the report, and owns program risk. The assessed organization supports the assessment and prepares the MMP where targets are missed.

**Contents list wins over older file maps.** A 2022 eight-file layout no longer matches Version 2025. Reconstruct against the current contents list, not against the prior pack basenames.

## Key Takeaways

- Version 2025 is advisory best practice for manufacturing maturity and risk.
- Responsible organization versus assessed organization is the deskbook role pair.
- Justification is removed; do not bring it back.
- Body sections 1 through 6 plus appendices B, C, and D are the reconstructable chapters; Appendix A stays a matrix reference; Appendix E folds into the glossary.
- Name DoDI 5000.88, FAR, and DFARS where relevant. Do not quote them.
