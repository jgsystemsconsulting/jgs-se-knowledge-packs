# Cheatsheet — NASA-STD-8719.14C

## When this pack applies
- NASA or NASA-sponsored object above ~130 km that reaches orbital velocity.
- Need ODAR/EOMP content, disposal option selection, or debris-limit shalls.

## Requirement numbers (memory aid)
| ID | Limit |
|----|--------|
| 4.3-1a | Released ≥1 mm LEO lifetime ≤25 y |
| 4.3-1b | <100 object-years per stage or spacecraft |
| 4.3-2 | ≥5 mm GEO-band exit in 25 y; stay out 100 y |
| 4.4-1 | P(explosion) < 0.001 per free flyer |
| 4.4-2 | Passivate or equivalently control stored energy |
| 4.4-3 / 4.4-4 | Intentional breakup: 100 object-years / 1 y; 10⁻⁶ in 24 h |
| 4.5-1 | P(>10 cm collision) < 0.001 over life (≤100 y storage) |
| 4.5-2 | P(MMOD kills disposal) < 0.01 during mission |
| 4.6-1 | Natural ≤25 y / direct reentry / retrieve ≤5 y |
| 4.6-2 | Storage / Earth escape band rules |
| 4.6-3 | Long-term reentry ≤200 y + zone dwell + 0.001 collision |
| 4.6-4 | Disposal reliability ≥ 0.90 (plus 4.7-1c if controlled) |
| 4.7-1 | 15 J; 1:10,000; controlled keep-outs |
| 4.8-1 | Special classes + ODMSP Objective 5 |

## Disposal preference
1. Immediate removal (direct reentry or Earth escape)
2. Fast LEO decay (as short as practicable, ≤25 y)
3. Justified storage or resonant long-term reentry

## Tells and smells
- **Smell**: First debris analysis at Final ODAR.
- **Smell**: “25 years” treated as the design point.
- **Smell**: Leak-before-burst cited as passivation.
- **Smell**: DAS used as a shielding or unofficial-reentry shop.
- **Tell**: Initial ODAR at MCR with a named disposal family.
- **Tell**: EOMP updated after a single-string or extension decision.
