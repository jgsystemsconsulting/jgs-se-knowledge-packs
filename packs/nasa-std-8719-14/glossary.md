# Glossary — NASA-STD-8719.14C Orbital Debris Process

- **CARA** — Conjunction Assessment Risk Analysis team for robotic NASA missions; coordinates EOM/disposal burns with 18 SPCS. → ch05
- **Credible failure mode** — FMEA (or equivalent) mode with a quantitative probability exceeding 1 in 1,000,000. → ch04
- **DAS** — Debris Assessment Software; primary Earth-orbit compliance tool unless OSMA pre-approves an alternate. → ch02, ch03, ch06
- **DCA (Debris Casualty Area)** — Combined ground-risk area of surviving >15 J fragments interacting with a human silhouette. → ch06
- **Disposal** — EOM process that moves a space structure to an orbit or trajectory acceptable for debris limitation. → ch05
- **EOMP** — End of Mission Plan; living operations document for passivation and disposal. → ch02, ch07
- **GEO protection zone** — GEO altitude ±200 km and ±15° inclination reserved for operating GEO spacecraft. → ch03, ch05
- **Large constellation** — 100 or more operational spacecraft cumulative (2019 ODMSP). → ch07
- **LEO** — Mean altitude ≤2000 km, or period ≤127 minutes. → ch03, ch05
- **Mission-related debris (MRD)** — Objects released during normal operations (covers, fittings, sub-1U satellites); not the intact bus or stage. → ch03
- **ODAR** — Orbital Debris Assessment Report (Initial / PDR / CDR / Final). → ch02, ch07
- **ODMSP** — U.S. Government Orbital Debris Mitigation Standard Practices (November 2019). → ch01, ch05, ch07
- **ODPO** — NASA Orbital Debris Program Office (JSC). → ch02
- **Orbital debris** — Artificial objects ≥1 mm left in orbit that no longer serve a useful purpose. → ch01
- **ORSAT** — Object Reentry Survival Analysis Tool; higher-fidelity reentry demise/casualty model. → ch06
- **Passivation** — Removing stored electrical, pressure, mechanical, or chemical energy that could later create debris. → ch04
- **Postmission disposal** — Intentional orbit change after the operational mission. → ch05
- **Tether** — Flexible structure longer than 300 m (yo-yo cables <10 m are MRD, not tethers). → ch07
