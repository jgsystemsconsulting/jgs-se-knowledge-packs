# FAA AMS Lifecycle V&V Guidelines — Glossary

Key terms from the FAA AMS Lifecycle Verification and Validation Guidelines, Version 4.0 (January 2023), alphabetical, with chapter references. Definitions are synthesized and paraphrased, never verbatim source text. The core verification and validation concepts trace to CMMI for Development, Version 1.2 (Carnegie Mellon Software Engineering Institute, August 2006); the guideline names that model in a page 3 footnote, and this pack paraphrases it. Method definitions from Appendix C are paraphrased from the guideline's own glossary.

**Accreditation** — formal certification that a given test capability is acceptable for a specific application; appears only in the product/component verification method set. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Acquisition Categories (ACAT)** — the FAA tiers of acquisition program; category drives which work products are required and therefore which get V&V'd. (`ch02`)

**Acquisition Management System (AMS)** — the FAA's policy framework of lifecycle phases, decision points, and stakeholder roles that this entire guideline wraps around. (`ch01`, `ch03`)

**Acquisition Program Baseline (APB)** — establishes the performance, cost, and schedule baselines for an investment program or segment; one of the five primary management documents, one of the four Appendix B checklist documents, and a V&V target in Final Investment Analysis. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Air Traffic Organization (ATO)** — the FAA organization whose Office of Safety and Technical Training joins the stakeholder list for safety and security V&V at the In-Service Decision and for ISM changes. (`ch04`)

**Analysis (method)** — comparing hardware or software design against known scientific and technical principles, technical data, or established practice to confirm the design will meet specified functional or performance requirements. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**AMS Rational Model (ARM)** — a static visualization of AMS work product relationships (the guideline also calls it the AMS Relational Model); a contextual relationship diagram, not a process flow, letting a user trace any acquisition document back to parents or forward to dependents. An interactive version is available on FAST. (`ch03`)

**Audit (method)** — independent examination of a work product, or of system records and activities, to verify conformance to specifications, standards, or policy; also used to test the adequacy of data security and data integrity procedures. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Business Case (BC)** — summarizes the business case analysis and supplies the analytical and quantitative basis for investment decisions; the fifth primary management document, V&V'd by Investment Planning & Analysis and closed by the IER, deliberately outside the Appendix B checklist set. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Checklist (method)** — a list of items to be verified, checked, or inspected; also the Appendix B instrument for the PRD, APB, ISPD, and PMP. (`ch02`, `ch03`, `ch04`)

**Concept and Requirements Definition (CRD)** — the AMS phase translating priority needs in the EA into preliminary requirements and a solution ConOps, quantifying the shortfall, and identifying candidate alternatives (one must be the EA's). (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Concept and Requirements Definition Readiness Decision (CRDRD)** — the SASP exit gate; Table 1's decision point. (`ch03`)

**Contractor Acceptance and Inspection (CAI)** — the Solution Implementation acceptance point where the system/service is V&V'd (major activities: DT, OT, IOA) ahead of production and in-service decisions. (`ch04`)

**Critical Design Review (CDR)** — the technical milestone at which structured V&V of the product baseline is a named opportunity. (`ch04`)

**Critical Operational Issue (COI)** — an operational issue carried in the final PRD; part of the validation criteria for the fielded system at SI acceptance and ISM changes. (`ch04`)

**Critical Performance Requirement (CPR)** — a value-bearing requirement identified in the PRD and carried into the APB; part of the validation criteria at SI and ISM. (`ch04`)

**CMMI for Development, Version 1.2** — Capability Maturity Model Integration for Development (Carnegie Mellon Software Engineering Institute, August 2006), named on the guideline's page 3 footnote as the model its V&V definitions are consistent with; also cited as a basis GAO uses for quality audits. Third-party copyrighted; this pack paraphrases and never copies CMMI text. (`ch01`)

**Demonstration (method)** — the operation of a system or component under controlled conditions to verify that it satisfies design requirements or to validate that it will fulfill its intended purpose. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Development Test (DT)** — developer-conducted, FAA-witnessed testing serving verification objectives: specified technical and performance requirements are met, and for final products, full integration, stability, and no adverse effect on the rest of the NAS. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Discussions with users (method)** — structured conversations with users used to validate work products and products against intended use. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Enterprise Architecture (EA)** — defines the operational and technical framework for all FAA capital assets: current and target architectures plus the transition strategy; a major V&V source criterion, itself verified and validated, and updated from V&V results. (`ch01`, `ch03`, `ch04`)

**FAA Acquisition System Toolset (FAST)** — the FAA acquisition system where the latest guideline version and the interactive ARM are published; this pack publishes no download link. (`ch01`, `ch03`)

**Final Investment Analysis** — the IA stage developing detailed plans and final requirements for the selected option; produces the program baseline work products (final PRD, final BC, final ISPD, APB, PMP, initial TEMP, tailored ISR checklist, SIR). (`ch04`)

**FIPS-199** — the federal standard whose Security Categorization Table is a validation criterion for ISS security assessments from SASP through CRD. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Functional presentation (method)** — a presentation illustrating the hierarchical arrangement of functions and interfaces, giving a complete performance and behavioral view of the system. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Independent Evaluation Review (IER)** — the Investment Planning and Analysis review that satisfies the documentation and reporting requirements for the Business Case's V&V. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Independent Operational Assessment (IOA)** — for designated products, an independent determination of operational readiness led by the program manager and an Independent Safety Assessment Team, supporting deployment decisions such as the ISD. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Independent stakeholder / reviewer** — a stakeholder or reviewer not directly involved in developing the work product, component, or product under review; named in Tables 1 through 6, and required for every V&V activity. (`ch02`, `ch03`, `ch04`)

**Initial Investment Analysis** — the IA stage evaluating alternatives and producing the initial PRD, initial ISPD, initial BC, preliminary TEMP, and the plan for Final IA. (`ch04`)

**Information Security Guidance for System Acquisition (ISGSA)** — the verification criterion for ISS security assessments from SASP onward. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Information System Security (ISS) assessment** — the family of security artifacts (Risk Factors, Preliminary, Initial) validated against FIPS-199 and verified against ISGSA, entering at the very first phase. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**In-Service Decision (ISD)** — the Solution Implementation gate into sustainment; supported by the completed ISR checklist, security authorization, and safety V&V. (`ch04`)

**In-Service Management (ISM)** — the AMS phase operating, maintaining, securing, and sustaining the fielded capability, and governing every change to it. (`ch04`)

**In-Service Review (ISR) checklist** — Final IA produces a tailored ISR checklist; the completed checklist is itself assessed at the ISD, with issues tracked question by question. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Implementation Strategy and Planning Document (ISPD)** — defines the overall implementation strategy and planning for the investment; a primary management document, an Appendix B checklist document, produced in initial and final versions, and updated again in ISM. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Inspection (method)** — verification by visual observation, mechanical measurement, or technical examination of engineering-support documentation, confirming compliance with specification requirements. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Integrated Baseline Review (IBR)** — the FAA process around contract award in SI that revalidates plans and baselines so they serve as the management construct for implementation. (`ch04`)

**Investment Analysis (IA)** — the AMS phase deciding what to build and committing resources; flexible and tailorable, with tailoring approved by the Acquisition Executive or IDA and recorded in the plan and the record of decision. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Investment Analysis Readiness Decision (IARD)** — the CRD exit gate into Investment Analysis; Table 2's decision point. (`ch04`)

**Investment Analysis Team** — the multi-specialist team defined in AMS Policy Section 2.5.1; a recurring stakeholder across IA tables, often joined by ANG Engineering Services and the ANG-E Test Standards Board. (`ch04`)

**Investment Decision Authority (IDA)** — the authority that receives IA options and approves IA tailoring. (`ch04`)

**Modeling (method)** — constructing programs that model the effects of a postulated environment, used to investigate a problem's dimensions or the effects of algorithmic processes. (`ch02`)

**NAS Change Proposal (NCP)** — the change-control instrument for modifications to the fielded NAS baseline; verified against the NAS Integrated Systems Engineering Framework, FAA Form 1800-2 (Case File / NCP form), and FAA Order 1800.66 configuration management policy. (`ch04`)

**NARP** — National Aviation Research Plan (the body text also renders it "National Air Research Plan"); a validation criterion for shortfall analyses where applicable. (`ch02`, `ch03`)

**National Airspace System (NAS)** — the FAA's operational system-of-systems that AMS investments deliver into and sustain. (`ch01`, `ch02`, `ch04`)

**NAS Segment Implementation Plan (NSIP)** — the segment-level implementation plan; a critical technical document and a recurring validation criterion. (`ch02`, `ch03`, `ch04`)

**Operational Test (OT)** — validation-side testing run by the OT Test Director and an OT test team of Air Traffic, Technical Operations, and second-level maintenance personnel; other FAA organizations and external entities may participate as required. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Peer review (method)** — a structured, methodical examination of a completed draft for quality improvement, conducted by unbiased subject matter experts independent of the item's development and approval (paraphrased from Appendix C). (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Preliminary Design Review (PDR)** — the technical milestone at which structured V&V of the allocated baseline is a named opportunity. (`ch04`)

**Plan of Actions and Milestones (POA&M)** — the instrument via which System Security Authorization is validated at the ISD. (`ch04`)

**Program Management Plan (PMP)** — defines how the service team will manage the investment program to execute the ISPD's strategy; a primary management document and an Appendix B checklist document. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Program Requirements Document (PRD)** — defines the operational framework and performance requirements the investment must achieve; produced as preliminary, initial, and final (FPRD) versions and V&V'd at each stage. (`ch03`, `ch04`)

**Quality Assurance (QA)** — a complementary process with a different purpose: QA confirms that established processes were followed; V&V confirms that requirements are met, user/mission needs are served, and risk is managed (paraphrased, CMMI-traced footnote content). (`ch01`)

**Research Service Analysis (RSA)** — a non-phase activity that precedes or runs during the early phases, maturing and validating new concepts and technologies for NAS investment; shares its V&V reviewers with SASP, CRD, IA, and early SI. (`ch03`)

**Safety Management System (SMS)** — the FAA safety regime; FAA SMS is a verification criterion for safety documentation (Safety Risk Management document or System Safety Assessment Report) in Solution Implementation. (`ch04`)

**Screening Information Request (SIR)** — the contract-facing solicitation artifact produced in Final IA (Statement of Work, Contract Data Requirements List / Data Item Descriptions, System Specification), V&V'd with the Contracting Officer. (`ch04`)

**Service Analysis & Strategic Planning (SASP)** — the AMS phase determining the capabilities that must be in place now and in the future, captured in EA as-is/to-be states and roadmaps. (`ch03`)

**Simulation (method)** — a model that behaves or operates like a given system under controlled inputs. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Solution Implementation (SI)** — the AMS phase building, buying, testing, and fielding the selected solution, from Contract Award through the In-Service Decision and deployment to all sites. (`ch04`)

**Storyboarding (method)** — a graphic arrangement of illustrations or images displayed in sequence to visualize a story or concept; a work-product validation method. (`ch02`)

**Subject Matter Expert (SME)** — practitioners the V&V Practice Toolkit offers advice for, and the unbiased performers of peer reviews. (`ch01`, `ch02`)

**System Security Authorization** — the security artifact validated via the POA&M and verified against the Security Authorization Handbook and AMS policy at the In-Service Decision. (`ch04`)

**Test and Evaluation (T&E)** — the primary product and product-component V&V approach, concentrated in SI and ISM, and divided into DT, OT, and IOA. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Test and Evaluation Master Plan (TEMP)** — the program-level T&E planning document, produced in preliminary, initial, and final versions and verified against the Test and Evaluation Handbook, the TEMP template, and AMS policy, with the ANG-E Test Standards Board on the stakeholder list. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Testing (method)** — operating a system or component under specified conditions, observing or recording results, and evaluating some aspect of it. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**User survey / user evaluation questionnaire (method)** — gathering information from individuals (printed questionnaire, telephone, mail, in person, or web) to validate against user needs. (`ch02`, `ch04`)

**Validation** — confirming the right thing was built: the product or end product component will fulfill its intended purpose and user needs in its intended environment; applied to the product, components, and selected work products, chosen as the best predictors of success (paraphrased; CMMI-traced, `ch01`). (`ch01`, `ch02`, `ch03`, `ch04`)

**Verification** — confirming the thing was built right: selected work products meet their specified requirements, incrementally from initial requirements through every change to the completed product (paraphrased; CMMI-traced, `ch01`). (`ch01`, `ch02`, `ch03`, `ch04`)

**V-Model** — the guideline's Figure 3: the left side vets strategic-planning work products, the right side tests and evaluates the integrated result, and loopback arrows show V&V against criteria in previous work products; captioned as shift-left V&V with evolving NAS capabilities. (`ch01`)

**Walk-through / dry run (method)** — rehearsing a process or procedure before real performance, deliberately mitigating the effects of possible failure; a work-product validation method. (`ch02`)

**Work product** — anything that represents, defines, or directs the final product: documents, processes, procedures, designs, descriptions, specifications, simulations, models, prototypes, contracts. (`ch01`, `ch02`)

**Verification and Validation (V&V)** — the disciplined, lifecycle-wide assessment of products, product components, and work products so that quality is built in and the built thing satisfies operational requirements and service needs. (`ch01`, `ch02`, `ch03`, `ch04`)
