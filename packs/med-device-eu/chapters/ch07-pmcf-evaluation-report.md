# How the PMCF evaluation report closes the plan, per MDCG 2020-8

S5 is the counterpart template to the PMCF plan.
The two documents travel together: the notified body's assessment of the clinical evaluation covers both the plan and the report against it.
The manufacturer analyses the findings from the planned activities and documents them in the PMCF evaluation report.
The report becomes part of the clinical evaluation report and the technical documentation.
Its job is reconciliation and routing: every planned activity accounted for, every finding routed to the document it affects.

The report binds itself to the plan it closes.
The header records the PMCF plan number and version the report answers to, plus the report's own number, date, version, and revision history.
A reader can therefore audit the closure against a named, versioned plan rather than against a moving target.

Section A repeats the manufacturer contact details: legal manufacturer, address, single registration number, the person responsible for regulatory compliance, and the authorised representative where one exists.
Section B repeats the device specification from the plan.
The template says to refer back to the plan's section B when nothing changed, and to fill the fields in again, highlighting the changes, when something did.
The fields match the plan's: product name, model, general description, intended purpose, intended users, Basic UDI-DI, patient population, medical conditions, indications, contraindications, warnings, variants, accessories, certificate number, classification, expected lifetime, and whether the product or a related clinical procedure is novel.

Section C is the reporting core.
The manufacturer reports every activity described in the plan's activity section that was performed, all the clinical data those completed activities collected, and a justification for any deviation from the plan.
Findings are discussed whether positive or negative.
The discussion also covers the potential impact on the documents reviewed during conformity assessment: the clinical evaluation report, the risk management file, the SSCP, and related documents.
Each activity type (device registry, PMCF study, real-world evidence, use surveys) gets its own subsection.
Each subsection must describe the quality of the data collected, and the template points to the methodological appraisal section of the European clinical evaluation guide as a usable basis for that assessment.

Section D handles data on equivalent or similar devices.
The manufacturer reports the clinical data collected on each listed device, the references the results came from (publications or the device's technical documentation), and an analysis with conclusions.
The key question: would changes in the state of the art, or newly identified hazards, affect the benefit-risk determination, the clinical evaluation, or the PMCF plan?

Section E is the routing table.
Two documents are considered at minimum: the clinical evaluation report and the risk management file, each cited by date and version.
For each, the manufacturer lists the relevant information analysed and monitored, states the analysis outcome to be reported in the updated document, and ticks a box where nothing was relevant.
Where potentially relevant information was left out, the manufacturer describes why.

Section F confirms whether the collected clinical data still support adherence to the common specifications, harmonised standards, and guidance documents listed in the PMCF plan, restated with title, date, and version.

Section G concludes the report.
The overall findings are set against the aims the plan declared.
The conclusions feed the next clinical evaluation and the next round of risk management.
They state whether any preventive or corrective measures are needed, and they give input to the next PMCF plan.

S5 states the closure loop directly: "The conclusions of the PMCF evaluation report shall be taken into account to update eventually the clinical evaluation, the risk management documentation, the post market surveillance plan and the SSCP, if applicable."
That sentence is the point of the document: the plan produced evidence, the report judges it, and the judgment flows back into the four documents that carry the device's clinical story.
A report that leaves an activity unreported, a deviation unjustified, or a finding unrouted has not closed the plan the template set up.
