# Glossary: med-device-eu

The working terms of the pack, in the authors' words, each naming the chapter that teaches it.

- **CEAR (clinical evaluation assessment report)**: The record a notified body produces when it assesses a manufacturer's clinical evaluation, written to the MDCG 2020-13 template. Each section ends in a compliance verdict, and expert panels read the report on its own during consultation, so it must carry the evidence story in full. → ch05
- **clinical evaluation**: The staged assessment of clinical data showing that a device achieves its intended benefits and is safe against the state of the art. It runs from a plan through data identification and appraisal to a conformity conclusion, and it keeps running through the device's lifetime. → ch04
- **IVDR (In Vitro Diagnostic Regulation)**: Regulation (EU) 2017/746, the binding EU law for in vitro diagnostics, with its own track for software that provides diagnostic information from specimen examination. Software mixing IVD and device data follows whichever data source substantially drives the intended purpose. → ch01
- **legacy device**: A device CE marked under the old directives (MDD or AIMDD) that now faces conformity assessment under the MDR. Its directive-era clinical data carries over, but into a gap analysis against the MDR's narrower definitions and stricter equivalence rules, not as a repackaged evaluation. → ch04
- **MDCG (Medical Device Coordination Group)**: The committee of Member State representatives the MDR established, chaired by a Commission representative, whose guidance documents this pack reconstructs. Its documents are endorsed best practice rather than law, and each carries a not-legally-binding notice on its first page. → ch02
- **MDR (Medical Device Regulation)**: Regulation (EU) 2017/745, the binding EU law for medical devices, including software that qualifies as a device. The MDCG guidance sits under it as interpretation, and notified bodies work to that guidance in practice. → ch01
- **PMCF (post-market clinical follow-up)**: The continuous process that collects clinical data from real-world use to update the clinical evaluation and confirm long-term safety and performance. It runs as a controlled document pair: a plan that commits the activities, then an evaluation report that closes them. → ch06, ch07
- **PMS (post-market surveillance)**: The manufacturer's system for watching a device's real-world safety and performance for as long as it stays on the market. PMCF is its clinical arm, and complaint, vigilance, and registry data feed both. → ch06, ch07
- **SaMD (software as a medical device)**: Software a manufacturer intends for a medical purpose, so it counts as a device in its own right rather than as an accessory or a component of a hardware device. The intended purpose decides, not where the software runs or what platform hosts it. → ch02
- **UKCA (UK Conformity Assessed)**: The conformity mark for medical devices, including software, placed on the Great Britain market under UK MDR 2002. The MHRA guidance wants it visible on the app's landing page and in the app store listing, so buyers can see conformity before purchase. → ch08
