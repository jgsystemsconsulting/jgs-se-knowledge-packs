Airworthiness Certification Criteria (MIL-HDBK-516D)

Knowledge base from MIL-HDBK-516D, 29 May 2026, Airworthiness Certification Criteria. Use for design AW criteria and MoCs across systems engineering, structures, flight technology, propulsion, air vehicle subsystems, crew systems and aerial refueling, avionics electrical and E3, computer systems and software, maintenance, armaments and stores, materials, and aerial delivery. Guidance handbook only. Not a service AW authority, not civil FAA or EASA rules, not detailed performance specs.
