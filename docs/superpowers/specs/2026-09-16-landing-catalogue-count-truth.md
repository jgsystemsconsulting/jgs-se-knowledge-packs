# Landing catalogue count truth (P15): design spec

Date: 2026-09-16. Status: drafted for planning. Package: P15.

## Problem

The public landing section 06 and the catalogue still say 54 packs. Live inventory is 65 pack directories: 63 content packs plus 2 signposts (`omg-signpost`, `se-standards-signpost`). README's badge already says 63 packs, SKILLS.md says "63 packs (+2 signposts)", and packs.html says 65 skills. Only the landing section and its still asset drifted. The landing numbers were hand-counted when the page was built and went stale as packs landed, so the fix needs a gate, not just an edit.

## Codebase context

Verified 2026-09-16 against the working tree:

- `packs/` holds 65 dirs. Exactly 2 SKILL.md files carry frontmatter `kind: signpost`; the other 63 are content packs. The `kind: signpost` marker is unique to those two files.
- `catalog.json` has a 63-slug `packs` array that is set-equal to the 63 content dirs (checked programmatically, zero diff in either direction). Signposts are not catalog entries.
- Stale surfaces: `docs/index.html:226` (h2 "54 packs · 2 signposts"), family chips at `docs/index.html:228-235` (hand-sum 54, and the EU/academic/JGS chip counts both signposts inside its 4), FIG.06 figcaption at `docs/index.html:241` ("Fifty-four packs"), `docs/assets/still-catalogue.svg:12` (subtitle), lines 13-22 (family rows), line 24 (footer).
- Already-correct surfaces: README badge (63 packs), SKILLS.md line 9, `docs/packs.html` title and header ("65 skills", generated by `tooling/gen_packs_page.py`).
- Gate: `tooling/check_release.py` runs numbered checks (1 through 12 today) and passes at v1.20.0. Check 12 covers `[html-assets]` and `[brand-tokens]`. `.github/workflows/validate.yml` inlines stdlib twins for deployed-page checks and never executes repo code; its header comment is the coverage map. `tooling/test_ci_gate.py` pins regex literal parity between the local gate and the workflow and runs negative demos.

## Decision: one landing convention

Landing wording: **63 packs · 2 signposts**.

- "Pack" on the landing page means a content pack reconstituted from a vetted source. Signposts are download pointers with no source content and are counted separately. This matches README and SKILLS.md.
- `packs.html` keeps "65 skills" and stays untouched. It lists every installable skill including signposts, it is generated, and its number already comes from the same generator that feeds check 6.
- Convention in one line: pack = content, skill = anything installable. The landing page needs no footnote because the headline already separates the two numbers.

## Changes

### 1. docs/index.html section 06

- Line 226 h2: `63 packs &middot; 2 signposts` (HTML entity form of the middle dot).
- Lines 228-235 chips: one chip per content family in the recount table, **including a new OMB / cross-federal chip**, plus a separate **Signposts** chip (tenth chip if nine content families are present). Chip markup stays the existing shape: `<div class="pk"><b>LABEL &middot; COUNT</b><span>description</span></div>`. The integer COUNT is only the digits immediately after the separator in the `<b>` label, never digits inside the description span.
- Signposts chip: label contains `Signposts` (case-insensitive) and COUNT equals live signpost count; description treats them as download pointers, not packs.
- Line 241 figcaption: `FIG.06 · Sixty-three packs plus two signposts across open sources; filter the full list on packs.html.`

### 2. docs/assets/still-catalogue.svg and still-catalogue.png

- Line 12 subtitle: `63 packs  ·  2 signposts  ·  open sources only` (keep the existing double-space style).
- Lines 13-22 family rows: NASA 16, DoD / DAU / OSD 19, NIST 09, FAA 07, GAO 04, zero-padded as today. The SVG shows the top five families only; that is the existing design and it stays. **Row counts are review-only** (not pinned by check 13); they must match the recount table at edit time.

### 3. tooling/check_release.py: new check 13 [catalogue-count]

Fail-closed equality check. Live counts come from the filesystem, never from a hardcoded constant:

- Inventory rule: every immediate child directory of `packs/` **must** contain a `SKILL.md` with parseable YAML frontmatter between `---` fences. Missing SKILL.md or unparseable frontmatter fails the check. Signpost detection (stdlib, same in local gate and CI twin): after the opening `---`, a line matching `^kind:\s*signpost\s*$` (case-insensitive) marks a signpost; any other pack is content. Content count N and signpost count M are the sizes of those two sets.
- Landing parse: slice `docs/index.html` from the `<!-- §06 The catalogue -->` marker to the next section comment so numbers from other sections cannot match. The h2 must match `<N> packs <sep> <M> signposts`, entity-aware (`&middot;` or the literal middle dot). Require N equal to live content count and M equal to live signpost count.
- Chip sum: for each `<div class="pk">` in the slice, parse only the integer in the `<b>... · COUNT</b>` (or middot) label pattern. Sum of content-chip COUNTs must equal N. Digits inside `<span>` descriptions are ignored. **Exactly one** chip whose `<b>` label contains `signpost` (case-insensitive) must exist, and its COUNT must equal M; that chip is excluded from the content sum.
- Figcaption: the §06 figcaption must contain the word-form or digit form of N and M consistent with the headline (at minimum the digits N and M, or the spelled forms used in the prescribed caption). Prefer matching the prescribed caption string after normalize whitespace.
- SVG parse: subtitle and footer of `still-catalogue.svg` must carry the same N and M (**case-insensitive** on the words packs/signposts; digits exact). SVG family **row** counts are not pinned.
- Any mismatch, missing file, or unparseable text fails with the live versus stated numbers in the message. Equality holds both directions.
- Update the module docstring to number the check (13) and keep its CI-coverage note accurate.

### 4. CI twin in .github/workflows/validate.yml

Required by the repo's own pattern: deployed-page content checks get CI twins (`[html-assets]` and `[brand-tokens]` both have one), and the landing page ships from the repo without anyone running Python locally. Add an inline stdlib python step that recomputes live counts and parses index.html and the SVG, same posture as the existing twins (never executes repo code). Add one coverage-map line to the header comment: landing catalogue counts twins check_release 13.

### 5. Tests

- Extend `tooling/test_ci_gate.py` with the existing pin-plus-parity pattern: the new count regexes must appear verbatim in both `check_release.py` and `validate.yml`, plus a negative demo where a stale index.html count fails.
- Add `tooling/test_catalogue_count.py`: assert-based parser tests for h2 extraction (entity-aware), chip summing, signpost-chip exclusion, SVG subtitle and footer extraction, and frontmatter signpost detection, using inline fixture strings. No pytest fixtures. Runnable directly and under pytest, matching the existing `test_*.py` style.

## Out of scope

- P8 and P16 catalog.json work. This package reads catalog.json only as verification evidence; no schema or data edits.
- Reopening P9-P14.
- Version bump and CHANGELOG. Owned by the release process; no plugin.json, CHANGELOG, or RELEASE-INFO edits here, so check 4 stays untouched.

## Risks and residuals

- Chip **membership** (which slug sits in which family) is curation. The check enforces headline equality, labelled chip COUNTs, required signpost chip, figcaption N/M, and SVG subtitle/footer; it cannot tell whether `federal-bca` sits in the right family chip. A wrong membership that still sums to 63 passes. Review catches it.
- SVG top-five **row** family numbers are review-only, same posture.
- PNG freshness is not machine-checked because no rasteriser exists in CI. A stale PNG is a review failure only.
- Future family splits stay free. Pinned surfaces are N, M, chip COUNT sum, signpost chip presence, figcaption, SVG subtitle/footer.

## Success criteria

- index.html section 06 headline, family chips (with OMB and Signposts chips), and figcaption, plus the SVG subtitle and footer, all state 63 packs and 2 signposts consistently. SVG top-five rows match the recount table at edit time. The regenerated PNG matches the SVG. packs.html is byte-identical to today.
- `python tooling/check_release.py` passes with the `[catalogue-count]` check in its output.
- CI `validate.yml` is green with the twin step, and its coverage map names check 13.
- Negative demo: temporarily change the h2 to "62 packs" and confirm check 13 fails naming live versus stated counts; restore and confirm PASS.
- `tooling/test_catalogue_count.py` and `tooling/test_ci_gate.py` pass.
